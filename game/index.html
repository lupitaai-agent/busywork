How to play

← → Walk
Hold to walk. Leave a room on the right, once the way is free.
Shift Run
Hold it while you walk. Faster, for when someone has just looked away.
Space Hop
Over crates and low things. Works while you walk.
↓ Hide
Only on a glowing spot on the floor. You duck behind what is there and nobody can see you. Press ↑ or an arrow to come out.
E Use
Does what the button says right now: pick up, snip, work a knot, cut someone loose. When it is dim, there is nothing to use here.
F Hit
Only with a hammer, so only Rivet: crates, snarls and posts.
↑ Climb
At a rope or ladder, once it is there. Vines are only for Pim. ↓ takes you down again.
Tab Swap
When there are two of you, swap who you are. Nobody can do everything. The other one stays where you left them, which you sometimes need.
Q Call someone
Two of you fit on screen. Whoever you are now goes, and the next one comes in. The danger waits until they're here.
T Throw
Carrying a pot or a key? Hold T, move the spot with ← →, and let go. Rivet can lift a friend with E and throw them too.
Hop, and hop again
Tuck and Zip can hop up to a shelf's edge and pull themselves up. Zip and Pim can flip in the air and reach pipes. Zip and Ivy can catch ropes. On a rope, ← → swings and Space lets go.
↓ Roll
While you walk: Tuck and Zip get under low tape.
1 to 6
With three or more of you, go straight to someone: the numbers are at the top of the screen.
Olaf and Juno
Olaf's E reads the room: he shows what nobody else can see. Juno's E sends an official a letter, and an official has to stop and read it.
The red light
What a tangled figure can see. Stay out of it, or hide until it has gone past.
Caught?
You start that room again, straight away. Nothing is lost.

The bar at the bottom always shows what you can do: bright means now.
