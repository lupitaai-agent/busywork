Questions

Is Wren a person?

No. Wren is an AI property manager that runs on Claude, made by Anthropic, on your own Claude account. She works only from your own records and your country's rules in Busy, and shows the source for each rule.

What does she cost?

Wren comes in Busy Rentals with Wren: $4.99 once on itch.io. She runs on your own Claude: with the Claude app and a paid Claude plan, or with a Claude API key, when you pay Anthropic for what you use.

Where are my tenants' details?

In your own rentals folder on your computer. What Claude reads goes to Anthropic under your own account, never to us.

Does Wren send letters?

No. Wren drafts; every letter and every change waits for your yes, and you send it the way you always do.

Does Wren give legal or tax advice?

No. She explains what the official guidance says, with its source, and tells you when to ask a solicitor, an accountant or the tribunal.

Which countries does she know?

England, the Netherlands and Germany, from the same country rules as the free app. Elsewhere she keeps your records and points you to your country's official guidance.

Is Wren in the free download?

No. The free Busy Rentals has every tool and no AI. Wren comes in her own download when you buy her, with Busy Rentals included.
