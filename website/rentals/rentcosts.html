Questions

Does Busy say what is deductible?

No. Busy totals what you recorded. Which costs count, and how, is for your tax office's guidance or your accountant.

Does Busy file my tax return?

No. It prepares the figures; you or your accountant file.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no AI.
