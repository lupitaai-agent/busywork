Questions

How big may a deposit be?

England: at most 5 weeks' rent, or 6 weeks when the rent is £50,000 a year or more. The Netherlands: at most 2 months' bare rent. Germany: at most 3 months' rent without the service-charge advance. Busy shows the cap for your home.

Does Busy collect the rent?

No. Busy keeps the record. The rent goes from your tenant to you as it does now.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no AI.
