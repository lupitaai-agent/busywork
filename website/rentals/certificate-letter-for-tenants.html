The letter

A copy for your records Dear [tenants' names], With this letter you receive a copy of [the document: the gas safety record, the electrical safety report, the energy certificate] for [address of the home], dated [date]. Please keep it with your papers. Kind regards, [your name]

How to use it

  • Copy the letter and fill in the parts in [brackets].
  • Send it by email or registered post, and keep a copy with the date you sent it.
  • A template, not legal advice. For a large amount or a dispute, ask someone who knows the rules where you live.
  • In the app, the letter fills itself in with your details and keeps a copy where you will find it again.
