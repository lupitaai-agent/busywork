Questions

Are the letters legal documents?

No. They are plain, polite templates, not advice. For a notice with legal effect, use your country's official form or ask a professional.

Which languages?

English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. A letter starts in the home's country's language; you choose another for each tenant, and Busy remembers it.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no AI.
