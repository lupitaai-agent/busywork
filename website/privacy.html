Privacy 🔒

Short version: the tools never send us anything. Only if you join the Olaf waitlist do we get your email address. The free tools have no AI. If you use an expert (Olaf, Ivy, Juno, Wren or Sal, the paid editions), what it reads goes to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, under your own Claude account or key, and nowhere else. This website shows ads from Google, and Google uses cookies for that, but only after you agree.

The tools

The Busy tools run in your own browser. Everything you enter is saved in a folder you choose on your computer. (Older versions could also keep it inside the browser; they now offer to move it to a folder.) We have no server that receives this data, no account system and no analytics inside the tools.

Links you click inside the tools, for example to open Gmail or Outlook with a ready-made email, go straight to that service. Sponsor cards shown under the tools are part of the download; they load nothing from the internet.

The Busy apps for Mac and Windows (Busy Business, Busy Growth, Busy Home and Busy Games) work the same way: your files are saved in a folder on your computer (by default in Documents/Busy). The free apps have no AI and don’t connect to the internet at all; links you click open in your own browser.

AI: only in the paid editions, with Olaf, Ivy, Juno, Wren or Sal

The free tools have no AI. An expert runs on Claude in one of two ways, and you choose.

In your own Claude app. You add the expert to the Claude app you already have. Busy then writes a short briefing (where things stand today) into a folder called Claude inside your Busy folder, and your Claude app reads it and writes its prepared work back there. Busy changes your records only when you say yes. What your Claude app reads is handled by Anthropic under your own Claude account and its terms. Nothing goes to us.

With your own API key. Your key is stored only in the browser on that computer, never in your Busy folder, and is sent only to Anthropic.

When you use an expert with a key, your browser sends the request straight to Anthropic's API. A request contains what Olaf needs to answer you:

  • What Olaf sends: the marketing or project foundation, the skill it runs, your brand profile, what you type, and the files in Olaf's workspace that it reads while working (your brand documents, its memory, its finished work, anything you put in its inbox), and, when it asks for them, your sales numbers per month from your Busy invoices: counts of customers and revenue, not names or invoice details.
  • Web search and page reading are done by Anthropic's own tools. The websites Olaf looks up see Anthropic, not you.

Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy, which you agreed to when you created your key. You can read them at anthropic.com/legal/privacy. We never see these requests or their answers, and the cost is billed by Anthropic to your account.

Conversations with Olaf and its files are saved like everything else: in your business folder. Delete them in Olaf, or by deleting the files.

This website

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. In the EU and UK you are asked for permission first, and you can change your choice at any time with the privacy link Google shows.

You can turn off personalised ads in Google’s ad settings, and read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The demo on this website runs on a pretend business. What you change stays in that browser tab and is gone when you close it; nothing is sent to us.

Buying a paid edition or a game

The paid apps and the Busy Street games are sold on itch.io. The payment and the download happen there, under itch.io's own terms and privacy policy, and we never see your card. Like every seller, we are told about each sale, which can include the email address you bought with. We use it only to answer you if you write to us.

The Olaf waitlist

If you put your name on the Olaf waitlist, we keep your email address (and your business name, if you give it) to tell you when Olaf can be bought. That is all we use it for. The list is kept by MailerLite (mailerlite.com, a company in the EU), which also sends the email that Olaf can be bought. Use the unsubscribe link in our email, and we delete your details. We delete the list once checkout is open and everyone on it has been told.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you from the tools, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete there: it is all in your own folder or browser. For data processed by Anthropic or Google, use their privacy pages linked above.
