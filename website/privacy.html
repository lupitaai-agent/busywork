Privacy 🔒

Short version: the tools never send us anything. If you switch on the AI features, what you ask about goes from your browser to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, with your own key, and nowhere else. This website shows ads from Google, and Google uses cookies for that, but only after you agree.

The tools

The Busywork tools run in your own browser. Everything you enter is saved in a folder you choose on your computer, or inside your browser if you chose “just try it out”. We have no server that receives this data, no account system and no analytics inside the tools.

Links you click inside the tools, for example to open Gmail or Outlook with a ready-made email, go straight to that service. Sponsor cards shown under the tools are part of the download; they load nothing from the internet.

AI features: the Marketing Studio and Olaf

The AI features are off until you add your own Claude API key. Your key is stored only in the browser on that computer, never in your business folder, and is sent only to Anthropic.

When you use them, your browser sends the request straight to Anthropic's API. A request contains what the feature needs to answer you:

  • Marketing Studio: the marketing foundation, the playbook you picked, your brand profile, your house rules, and what you type.
  • Olaf: the same, plus the files in Olaf's workspace that it reads while working (your brand documents, its memory, its finished work, anything you put in its inbox), and, when it asks for them, your sales numbers per month from your Busywork invoices: counts of customers and revenue, not names or invoice details.
  • Web search and page reading (Olaf, and the Studio when you allow it) are done by Anthropic's own tools. The websites you or Olaf look up see Anthropic, not you.

Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy, which you agreed to when you created your key. You can read them at anthropic.com/legal/privacy. We never see these requests or their answers, and the cost is billed by Anthropic to your account.

Conversations with Olaf and its files are saved like everything else: in your business folder, or in your browser. Delete them in Olaf, or by deleting the files.
