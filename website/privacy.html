Privacy 🔒

Short version: the tools never send us anything. Only if you join the Olaf waitlist do we get your email address. The free tools have no AI. If you use Olaf, the paid edition, what you ask it goes from your browser to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, with your own key, and nowhere else. This website shows ads from Google, and Google uses cookies for that, but only after you agree.

The tools

The Busywork tools run in your own browser. Everything you enter is saved in a folder you choose on your computer. (Older versions could also keep it inside the browser; they now offer to move it to a folder.) We have no server that receives this data, no account system and no analytics inside the tools.

Links you click inside the tools, for example to open Gmail or Outlook with a ready-made email, go straight to that service. Sponsor cards shown under the tools are part of the download; they load nothing from the internet.

AI: only in Olaf, the paid edition

The free tools have no AI. Olaf uses Claude with your own API key. Your key is stored only in the browser on that computer, never in your business folder, and is sent only to Anthropic.

When you use Olaf, your browser sends the request straight to Anthropic's API. A request contains what Olaf needs to answer you:

  • What Olaf sends: the marketing or project foundation, the skill it runs, your brand profile, what you type, and the files in Olaf's workspace that it reads while working (your brand documents, its memory, its finished work, anything you put in its inbox), and, when it asks for them, your sales numbers per month from your Busywork invoices: counts of customers and revenue, not names or invoice details.
  • Web search and page reading are done by Anthropic's own tools. The websites Olaf looks up see Anthropic, not you.

Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy, which you agreed to when you created your key. You can read them at anthropic.com/legal/privacy. We never see these requests or their answers, and the cost is billed by Anthropic to your account.

Conversations with Olaf and its files are saved like everything else: in your business folder. Delete them in Olaf, or by deleting the files.

This website

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. In the EU and UK you are asked for permission first, and you can change your choice at any time with the privacy link Google shows.

You can turn off personalised ads in Google’s ad settings, and read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The demo on this website runs on a pretend business. What you change stays in that browser tab and is gone when you close it; nothing is sent to us.

The Olaf waitlist

If you put your name on the Olaf waitlist, we keep your email address (and your business name, if you give it) to tell you when Olaf can be bought. That is all we use it for. The list is kept by MailerLite (mailerlite.com, a company in the EU), which also sends the email that Olaf can be bought. Ask us at the address below to be removed, or use the unsubscribe link in our email, and we delete your details. We delete the list once checkout is open and everyone on it has been told.
