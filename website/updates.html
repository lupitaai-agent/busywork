Busy 0.9.7 · 2026-11-10

Busy Business starts with your brand

  • Busy Business opens on your brand: a brand card first, then your tools in three groups: Grow, Deliver and Run.
  • In Busy Home's Just in Case binder, a contract or a document now opens in its own tool when you click it.
  • Safer apps: a file Busy opens for you is only ever a document or a picture, and two windows can't trip over the same file.
  • The website says plainly what is free and what will be paid: the tools stay free, and a paid version with an AI expert is coming.

Busy 0.9.6 · 2026-11-06

A new free game: Busy Street: Tuck's Errand

  • Tuck only went out to get a stamp back. Six levels, six friends to cut loose from the Red Tape.
  • Play it in your browser, or download it for Mac and Windows with a film at the start and end of every level.
  • The AI experts are coming later, so the site no longer shows prices for them.

Busy 0.9.5 · 2026-11-03

Puzzles in their room, and every screen in your language

  • Play the puzzles in their room: on each game, tap In the room for a real wooden board in the Puzzle Parlour.
  • Every place opens with a short film, and follows the seasons: spring, summer, autumn and winter.
  • Rooms that follow your own work: boxes on the stock shelves, unpaid invoices in the pigeonholes, a leaf on the trellis for every skill, and a drawer label that glows before a document expires.
  • Every screen now speaks your language, Olaf, Ivy and Juno included.

Busy 0.9.4 · 2026-11-02

Busy speaks your language

  • Busy now speaks Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese as well as English.
  • Local rules for each country: the right currency and VAT rates, invoices and reminders in six languages, and letters that name your country's law.

Busy 0.9.3 · 2026-11-01

Your guide is alive

  • Rivet, Pim and Tuck now stand in the room on your home screen, and turn their head to follow you.
  • The rooms follow the clock: in the morning, by day or in the evening, with the light to match.

Busy 0.9.2 · 2026-11-01

A guide who says what needs you

  • Your guide says one line on the home screen about what needs you most, like the last day to cancel a contract or an overdue invoice.
  • The rooms look better, with proper objects: the van, the counter, the glasshouse door, the jar of wins.

Busy 0.9.1 · 2026-10-31

Every app has its own place

  • Each app opens in its own place: the Workshop, the Glasshouse, Tuck's house and the Puzzle Parlour.
  • Every tool has its own room, and the top bar jumps straight to any other tool.

Busy 0.9.0 · 2026-10-27

Busy as free apps for Mac and Windows

  • Busy Business, Busy Growth, Busy Home and Busy Games as free apps for Mac and Windows, one for each part of your life.
  • Your files stay in your own folder as plain files. No account, nothing sent to us.
