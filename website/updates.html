Busy Kitchen 1.0 · 2026-11-18

Bring the world to your table.

  • 1,886 dishes from six kitchens, matched to what's in your fridge. The home cooking of Busy's six languages, with each kitchen's story, regions and feast days, and a recipe for every dish.
  • Tonight Type what is in your fridge, in any of the six languages, and see what you can cook now, and what needs one thing more. Or travel tonight to one kitchen.
  • The six kitchens Each kitchen's story, how to say its dishes, its regions, its pantry, how people eat, its calendar and its dishes, with pictures.
  • Food calendar What the six kitchens cook through the year: Sinterklaas, Advent, Semana Santa, Natale, Festa Junina, Thanksgiving and every season.
  • Recipe Box Your own recipes and the family's, with a photo of the handwritten card, the story behind it, and a family cookbook to print.
  • Week Plan & Shopping The next two weeks' dinners and the leftovers, and the shopping list that follows, on your phone through your synced folder or printed.
  • Pantry What is in the fridge, the freezer and the cupboard, with use-by dates. What goes off first comes first.
  • Busy wrote every recipe in its own words, from how the dish is made at home in its region, and checked each one against written rules: the amounts, the oven, the allergens and how to know meat is cooked through. Nobody at Busy has cooked them all yet, and each recipe says so. Tell us how it went, and the recipes get better from what you tell us.
  • Nothing. Busy Kitchen is free, with no account and no AI. Busy Kitchen with Sal, your chef, is $1.49 once on itch.io, and Sal works on your own Claude.

Busy Rentals 1.0 · 2026-11-17

Be the landlord people hope for.

  • Rental management for people who let one home or a few: the dates, the papers, the rent and the tax year, in one place.
  • Homes & Tenancies Every home you let, who lives there, and every check and certificate with its date and its official source.
  • Rent & Deposits What came in, what is late, and where each deposit stands: the cap, and the date to protect or return it.
  • Letters to Tenants A welcome, a visit, a repair, a copy of a certificate and the deposit at the end, in six languages: you choose for each tenant.
  • Costs & the Tax Year Costs per home with the receipt, and each home's totals for the year, ready for your return or your accountant.
  • England, the Netherlands and Germany: each duty Busy keeps, with how often it comes back, its official page and the date Busy last checked it. In any other country, Busy keeps your homes, rent, deposits, costs and letters, without country dates.
  • Busy Rentals is free, with no account and no subscription. Wren, the AI property manager, comes in the paid edition: Busy Rentals with Wren, $4.99 once on itch.io.

Busy 2.1 · 2026-11-17

Busy Home 2.1: for a household of any size

  • The whole household Everyone who lives there, people and pets, each with a colour. Say who looks after what, and see just your own things.
  • Shared Costs Who paid what, and who owes whom: split equally, by income share or your own way, between any number of people.
  • Pets Vaccinations and check-ups with their next dates, a sitter's booklet as a PDF, and a checklist for travelling with a pet in the EU.
  • Tax time The date for the UK, the Netherlands, Germany, Spain, Italy or Brazil, the papers to gather, and a tag to keep a paper for the return.
  • Drop here A folder for the scans and photos from your phone: they wait in the tray to be filed.
  • The renewal check Last year's price beside this year's, and a reminder to compare energy deals before a fixed deal ends.
  • Free, like the rest. Juno, in the paid edition, does the boring part of each for you.

Busy 2.0 · 2026-11-15

Busy with what matters: every app opens on what needs you today

  • Each app now opens on Today: the three things that most need you, and nothing else. The tools sit behind a few clear parts: Brand, Marketing and Operations in Busy Business; My worth, Grow and Say it in Busy Growth; the tray, Dates, Letters and Just in case in Busy Home.
  • Busy Business has your brand on one page and a road for every job, from quote to paid. Busy Growth lays out your notes for a review, the case for a raise and your thirty seconds. Busy Home has a tray for the post and one list of every date at home.
  • The experts have a clearer job: Olaf, your brand consultant; Ivy, your career coach; Juno, your household assistant. Each prepares the work and waits for your yes.
  • New in the paid apps: the expert works inside your own Claude app, on a paid Claude plan. You add it in three steps. A Claude API key still works.
  • Olaf can now read your invoices, quotes, clients and hours, and prepare a draft or a reminder for you to send.
  • The free tools stay free, with no AI and no account. Everything is in English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese.
