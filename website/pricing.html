Questions

Do the free tools stay free?

Yes. Every tool in every suite is free, with no account, no trial and no time limit. They have no AI in them.

What exactly do I buy?

The same app with its AI expert inside: Olaf in Busy Business, Ivy in Busy Growth, Juno in Busy Home. You pay once and keep it. Updates are free.

Why do I need a Claude key?

The expert is Claude, made by Anthropic. It runs on your own key, so your questions go straight from your computer to Anthropic and never to us. You pay Anthropic for what you use: a typical conversation costs a few cents. The app walks you through getting a key in about five minutes.

Why itch.io?

itch.io takes the payment and hands you the download, so we never see your card. The buttons on this site open the product's own page there in a new tab.

Are these the final prices?

They are introductory prices. If they change, people who already bought keep what they have.

Which languages?

English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese: the apps, the experts' own screens and both games.

Mac or Windows?

The three apps and Tuck's Errand run on Mac and Windows. Busy Street 3D is for Mac with Apple silicon. The apps are not signed yet, so your computer warns you the first time you open one.
