Questions

Do the free tools stay free?

Yes. Every tool in every suite is free, with no account, no trial and no time limit. They have no AI in them.

What exactly do I buy?

The same app with its AI expert inside: Olaf in Busy Business, Ivy in Busy Growth, Juno in Busy Home, Wren in Busy Rentals, Sal in Busy Kitchen. You pay once and keep it. Updates are free.

How does the expert run?

The expert is Claude, made by Anthropic, and works on your own Claude: the Claude app with a paid plan, or a Claude API key. If you already pay for Claude, the expert's price is all you pay: you add the expert to your Claude app in three steps. With an API key you pay Anthropic for what you use, a few cents a conversation. The expert prepares the work; you say yes in Busy. The free app is complete on its own.

Why itch.io?

itch.io takes the payment and hands you the download, so we never see your card. The buttons on this site open the product's own page there in a new tab.

Are these the final prices?

They are introductory prices. If they change, people who already bought keep what they have.

Which languages?

English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese: the apps, the experts' own screens and both games.

Mac or Windows?

The apps and Tuck's Errand run on Mac and Windows; the free Busy Kitchen opens in your browser. Busy Street 3D is for Mac with Apple silicon. The apps are not signed yet, so your computer warns you the first time you open one.
