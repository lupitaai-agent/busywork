Busy Kitchen in short

What is Busy Kitchen?

Busy Kitchen is a free app with the home cooking of six language worlds: 1,886 dishes from the Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and English kitchens, each with its story, its regions, its feast days and a recipe, in English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese.

Who is it for?

Busy Kitchen is for home cooks who want dinner from what is in the fridge, and for anyone curious how families in six language worlds eat: at home, on feast days and through the seasons.

How does it find dinner?

Type what is in your fridge, in any of the six languages. Busy Kitchen shows the dishes you can cook now, and the ones that need one or two things more. Salt, oil and the other basics are taken as read.

Are the recipes any good?

Busy wrote every recipe in its own words, from how the dish is made at home in its region, and checked each one against written rules: the amounts, the oven, the allergens and how to know meat is cooked through. Nobody at Busy has cooked them all yet, and each recipe says so. Tell us how it went, and the recipes get better from what you tell us.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Busy Kitchen is free, with no account and no AI. Busy Kitchen with Sal, your chef, is $1.49 once on itch.io, and Sal works on your own Claude.

Is there AI in the free version?

No. The free Busy Kitchen has no AI and sends nothing anywhere: it matches your fridge to the dishes on your own computer. Only Sal, in the paid edition, uses AI: Claude, on your own Claude account. The pictures of the kitchens' places and customs were made with AI, and the app says so beside them.

Where are my recipes?

In a folder you choose on your own computer, as plain files. Put it in Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or iCloud Drive and the shopping list reaches your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Which kitchens are in it?

The Dutch kitchen (the Netherlands, Flanders, and Surinamese and Indonesian-Dutch home cooking), the German (Germany, Austria and Switzerland), the Spanish (Spain and Latin America), the Italian (all twenty regions), the Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal) and the English (Britain, Ireland and the United States).

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. The Recipe Box keeps your own and your family's recipes, with a photo, the story and the country it came from. Every house recipe can go into it too, as your own copy to change. Print them all as a family cookbook.

What about allergies?

Each recipe lists what it contains from the EU's 14 allergens, hidden ones such as stock and soy sauce included. It never says a dish is free of anything. Always check the labels of what you buy.

What if I can't find an ingredient?

Each kitchen has its ingredients abroad: what to use instead, close and never quite the same. A missing ingredient in Tonight shows its swap.

Are the pictures real?

The pictures of the kitchens' places and customs are made with AI, to show each kitchen as it lives, and the app says so beside them.

Will the recipes keep changing?

No. The collection is fixed and complete. We improve a recipe when people tell us how it went.

Why six kitchens?

Busy speaks six languages, and each language has a kitchen behind it. So Busy Kitchen is a cultural kitchen: the dishes those languages grew up with, at home and on their feast days.
