Busy Kitchen · the six kitchens

What can I cook with what I have?

Find the line that looks like your fridge. Every dish below has a recipe in Busy Kitchen; type your own ingredients there, in any of six languages, and see what you can cook now.

🧊 Type what's in your fridge

Potatoes, onions and eggs

  • 🇩🇪 Labskaus · Labskaus (sailors' corned-beef hash)

    Where DE – Hamburg, Schleswig-Holstein, Bremen

    When Year-round, harbour dish

    An "old sailors' dish" from the age of sail, mashing preserved ship's stores into one pink hash.

    Key ingredients Salt or corned beef, potatoes, beetroot, onion; fried egg, rollmops, gherkin

    How it is eaten Topped with a fried egg, with rollmops and pickled gherkin on the side

  • 🇪🇸 Silpancho · Silpancho

    Where Bolivia: Cochabamba

    When Everyday

    The name comes from Quechua sillp'anchu ("thin, pounded"). Its invention is attributed to Celia la Fuente Peredo.

    Key ingredients Thin breaded beef, rice, potato, egg, tomato and onion salad

    How it is eaten Layered on one plate

  • 🇧🇷 Bacalhau à Gomes de Sá · Gomes de Sá salt cod

    Where Portugal, Porto

    When Everyday; festive

    One of the 21 finalists of 2011. Named after a Porto cod merchant.

    Key ingredients Flaked cod, potato, onion, egg, olives, olive oil

    How it is eaten From the oven dish

    Protected Cod: ETG Bacalhau de Cura Tradicional Portuguesa

  • 🇬🇧 Latkes

    Where USA (Jewish American)

    When Hanukkah

    Fried in oil to recall the Hanukkah oil miracle; potatoes replaced cheese in Eastern Europe.

    Key ingredients Potatoes, onion, egg, oil

    How it is eaten With apple sauce and sour cream

  • 🇩🇪 Reibekuchen / Kartoffelpuffer / Reiberdatschi · Potato pancakes

    Where DE – Rhineland (Reibekuchen), all

    When Autumn, Christmas markets, fairs

    The Cologne name is Reibekuchen, the Bavarian one Reiberdatschi.

    Key ingredients Grated raw potato, onion, egg; fried

    How it is eaten With apple sauce, or on dark bread with sugar beet syrup

  • 🇪🇸 Tortilla de patatas · Spanish potato omelette

    Where Nationwide

    When Every day; picnics

    First recorded in an 1817 memorial to the Navarre parliament about poor farmers. The version with onion is generally the more popular.

    Key ingredients Potatoes, eggs, olive oil, salt, onion (debated)

    How it is eaten Hot, warm or cold; in wedges or as a pincho

  • 🇧🇷 Bacalhau à Brás · Brás salt cod

    Where Portugal, Lisboa; national

    When Weeknights

    Shredded salt cod scrambled with egg and matchstick potatoes, said to be named after a Bairro Alto tavern keeper called Brás.

    Key ingredients Shredded cod, matchstick potatoes, egg, onion, olives, parsley

    How it is eaten From the pan

    Protected Cod ETG

Minced meat and potatoes

  • 🇳🇱 Blauwe Jan · Red-cabbage mash

    Where NL – national

    When Winter

    A red-cabbage stamppot.

    Key ingredients Potatoes, red cabbage, minced meat

    How it is eaten Hot, with gehakt or sausage

  • 🇬🇧 Shepherd's pie

    Where England (also Scotland, Ireland)

    When Monday after the roast, everyday

    A thrift dish for leftover roast meat; 'shepherd's pie' is used for lamb.

    Key ingredients Minced or leftover lamb, onion, carrots, gravy, mashed potato topping

    How it is eaten Baked until the mash browns; with peas

  • 🇳🇱 Perkedel / frikadel · Indische meat or potato patty

    Where Indisch (NL)

    When Rijsttafel

    Fried patties.

    Key ingredients Potato or mince, nutmeg

    How it is eaten Hot or cold

  • 🇬🇧 Cottage pie

    Where England

    When Everyday

    'Cottage pie' appears in print from the late 18th century, when potatoes were becoming a poor family's food; it is the beef version.

    Key ingredients Minced beef, onion, carrot, gravy, mashed potato

    How it is eaten Baked, with greens or peas

  • 🇬🇧 Pie and mash with liquor · Pie 'n' mash

    Where England (London, East End)

    When Everyday, Saturday

    Born in 19th-century London eel-and-pie shops; the green liquor was once made with eel stock. In 2024 MPs backed a motion calling for protected status.

    Key ingredients Minced beef pie, mashed potato, parsley 'liquor' sauce

    How it is eaten Mash scraped on the plate, liquor poured over; chilli vinegar

  • 🇬🇧 Mince and tatties

    Where Scotland

    When Everyday

    One of Scotland's most common family dinners.

    Key ingredients Minced beef, onion, carrot, potatoes

    How it is eaten Mince over boiled or mashed potatoes

    Protected Ingredient: Scotch beef PGI

Chicken and rice

  • 🇪🇸 Nacatamal · Nicaraguan tamal

    Where Nicaragua

    When Weekends (Sunday breakfast), fiestas

    Nicaragua's version of the tamal.

    Key ingredients Maize masa with lard, pork or chicken, rice, potato, tomato, onion, banana leaf

    How it is eaten Unwrapped, with bread and coffee

  • 🇧🇷 Galinhada · Chicken rice with turmeric

    Where Brazil, CW (Goiás)

    When Sunday; parties

    Goiás's one-pot chicken rice.

    Key ingredients Chicken, rice, açafrão-da-terra (turmeric); pequi optional

    How it is eaten From the pot

  • 🇬🇧 Cock-a-leekie

    Where Scotland

    When Burns Night, St Andrew's Day, Hogmanay

    A peppered chicken and leek soup, recorded from the 16th century in Scotland in some form.

    Key ingredients Chicken, leeks, prunes, rice or barley

    How it is eaten In bowls, prunes optional

  • 🇪🇸 Tamal tolimense · Tolima tamal

    Where Colombia: Tolima

    When Sunday breakfast, Christmas

    Regional tamal.

    Key ingredients Masa, rice, peas, pork, chicken, egg, plantain leaf

    How it is eaten With hot chocolate and bread

  • 🇧🇷 Prato feito (PF) · Set lunch plate

    Where Brazil, national

    When Weekday lunch

    Everyday lunch of rice and beans, salad and a protein.

    Key ingredients Rice, beans, steak or chicken, salad, fries, egg

    How it is eaten One plate, at a lunch counter

  • 🇬🇧 Jerk chicken with rice and peas

    Where England (British Caribbean; London, Birmingham, Bristol)

    When Sunday dinner, Notting Hill Carnival (August bank holiday), Christmas

    Jamaican jerk has Maroon and Taíno roots; the Windrush generation, named after HMT Empire Windrush (arrived June 1948), made rice and peas a Sunday staple of British Caribbean homes.

    Key ingredients Chicken, allspice (pimento), Scotch bonnet, thyme; rice cooked with kidney beans and coconut milk

    How it is eaten With coleslaw, fried plantain, hot pepper sauce

  • 🇪🇸 Arroz chaufa · Peruvian fried rice

    Where Peru

    When Everyday

    A chifa (Chinese-Peruvian) classic.

    Key ingredients Rice, egg, scallion, soy sauce, chicken or pork

    How it is eaten As a main dish or side

  • 🇧🇷 Arroz de cabidela · Blood rice with chicken

    Where Portugal, Minho; national

    When Sunday

    The origin of Brazil's cabidela.

    Key ingredients Chicken, its blood, vinegar, rice

    How it is eaten Soupy rice in deep plates

Pasta and tomatoes

  • 🇳🇱 Macaroni met ham en kaas · Dutch macaroni bake

    Where NL – national

    When Weekday

    A common home dish; an Indische version exists.

    Key ingredients Macaroni, mince or ham, tomato, cheese

    How it is eaten Oven dish

  • 🇩🇪 Jägerschnitzel (Ost) · Breaded Jagdwurst slice

    Where DE – East (former GDR)

    When Year-round

    In the East, "Jägerschnitzel" meant a breaded slice of Jagdwurst sausage with macaroni and tomato sauce.

    Key ingredients Jagdwurst slice breaded and fried, tomato sauce, pasta

    How it is eaten With spirelli and tomato sauce, school-canteen style

  • 🇮🇹 Rancetto / spaghetti con le aringhe · Umbrian Christmas Eve spaghetti

    Where Italy / Umbria

    When Christmas Eve

    Served for the Vigilia in its herring version.

    Key ingredients Spaghetti, tomato, pancetta; herring for the Vigilia version

    How it is eaten Hot

  • 🇬🇧 Spaghetti and meatballs

    Where USA (Italian American)

    When Sunday dinner

    Italian immigrants could afford more meat in America, so meatballs grew and joined the pasta.

    Key ingredients Spaghetti, beef meatballs, tomato sauce

    How it is eaten Family-style

  • 🇮🇹 Fileja con 'nduja · Twisted pasta with 'nduja

    Where Italy / Calabria (Vibo Valentia)

    When All year

    Paired with the spicy spreadable salami of Spilinga.

    Key ingredients Hand-rolled pasta, 'nduja, tomato

    How it is eaten Hot

Eggs, cheese and bread

  • 🇳🇱 Uitsmijter · Fried eggs on bread

    Where NL – national

    When Lunch

    Served in bars and restaurants.

    Key ingredients Bread, fried eggs, ham, cheese

    How it is eaten Knife and fork, at lunch

  • 🇩🇪 Pinzgauer Kaspressknödel · Pinzgau pressed cheese dumplings

    Where AT – Salzburg (Pinzgau), Tyrol

    When Year-round, mountain huts

    Flattened and fried cheese dumplings, listed in the Austrian register.

    Key ingredients Stale bread, sharp mountain cheese (Pinzgauer Bierkäse or Graukäse), eggs, onion

    How it is eaten In beef broth or with sauerkraut or salad

  • 🇮🇹 Canederli (Knödel) · Bread dumplings

    Where Italy / Trentino-Alto Adige

    When All year, winter

    Big Tyrolean dumplings of stale bread.

    Key ingredients Stale bread, milk, eggs, speck or cheese and herbs

    How it is eaten In broth, or dry with melted butter and sage

    Protected Speck Alto Adige IGP (when used)

  • 🇧🇷 Francesinha · Francesinha sandwich

    Where Portugal, Porto

    When Weekends

    Created in 1953 by Daniel David da Silva at A Regaleira, inspired by the croque-monsieur.

    Key ingredients Bread, steak, ham, linguiça, sausage, cheese, spicy tomato sauce (traditionally with beer), egg

    How it is eaten Knife and fork, with fries

  • 🇩🇪 Käseschnitte / Chäsbrätel · Cheese toast

    Where CH – all

    When Winter, alp huts

    Bread spread with a cheese mixture, then baked or fried; a Swiss classic recorded since the 18th century, especially in Valais.

    Key ingredients Bread (soaked in white wine or milk), cheese, ham, egg

    How it is eaten From the oven with a fried egg

  • 🇮🇹 Pallotte cacio e ova · Cheese and egg balls in sauce

    Where Italy / Abruzzo

    When All year

    Fried, then simmered in tomato sauce.

    Key ingredients Bread, cheese, eggs, tomato sauce

    How it is eaten Hot

  • 🇮🇹 Casatiello · Easter lard bread with eggs

    Where Italy / Campania (Napoli)

    When Easter, Pasquetta

    Naples's savoury Easter bread.

    Key ingredients Bread dough, lard, salami, cheese, whole eggs

    How it is eaten Sliced, at picnics

Cabbage and sausage

  • 🇳🇱 Boerenkoolstamppot met rookworst · Kale and potato mash with smoked sausage

    Where NL – national

    When Winter

    The classic stamppot and the default meaning of "stamppot". Groningen calls it "mous".

    Key ingredients Curly kale, potatoes, bacon, rookworst, mustard

    How it is eaten Hot, mash with a well of gravy, sausage on top, mustard

  • 🇩🇪 Grünkohl mit Pinkel · Kale with Pinkel sausage

    Where DE – Bremen, Oldenburg, Lower Saxony

    When Winter, after the first frost; Kohlfahrt

    Eaten after "Kohlfahrten", winter walks that end with a kale dinner and a "Kohlkönig" being crowned.

    Key ingredients Kale, Pinkel (grain sausage), Kassler, bacon, Kochwurst

    How it is eaten With sugar-glazed or boiled potatoes

  • 🇪🇸 Pote asturiano · Asturian cabbage pot

    Where Asturias

    When Winter

    A pote (soup-stew) that is central to everyday cooking.

    Key ingredients White beans, cabbage, chorizo, lacón, potatoes

    How it is eaten Hot, from the pot

  • 🇮🇹 Cassoeula · Pork and savoy cabbage stew

    Where Italy / Lombardia

    When Winter

    Lombardy's most typical winter dish.

    Key ingredients Pork ribs, rind, trotters, sausages, savoy cabbage

    How it is eaten With polenta

  • 🇬🇧 Pierogi and kielbasa

    Where USA (Midwest, Pennsylvania; Polish American)

    When Christmas Eve, everyday

    Polish and Slavic immigrant foods now common in Pittsburgh, Chicago and Cleveland.

    Key ingredients Dough, potato and cheese, sauerkraut; smoked sausage

    How it is eaten Boiled then fried with onions

  • 🇳🇱 Zuurkoolstamppot · Sauerkraut mash

    Where NL – national

    When Winter

    Sometimes given a twist with curry powder, raisins, pineapple or banana.

    Key ingredients Sauerkraut, potatoes, rookworst or bacon

    How it is eaten Hot, with sausage and gravy

  • 🇩🇪 Berner Platte · Bernese platter

    Where CH – Bern

    When Autumn, feasts (Metzgete)

    Linked by legend to the celebration of the 1798 battle of Neuenegg.

    Key ingredients Sauerkraut, green beans, smoked and boiled meats, sausages, tongue

    How it is eaten A large shared platter with boiled potatoes

    Protected Berner Zungenwurst IGP

  • 🇪🇸 Cocido montañés · Mountain stew

    Where Cantabria

    When Winter; Easter Sunday

    A hearty Cantabrian stew. It is eaten on Easter Sunday when meat abstinence ends.

    Key ingredients White or red beans, cabbage, pork rib, morcilla, chorizo, bacon

    How it is eaten Hot, single pot

Beans and rice

  • 🇳🇱 BB met R (bruine bonen met rijst) · Brown beans with rice

    Where Surinaams (NL)

    When Weekday

    Popularised in the Netherlands by Max Woiski sr. and jr.

    Key ingredients Brown beans, rice, salted meat

    How it is eaten Hot

  • 🇪🇸 Pupusas · Pupusas

    Where El Salvador

    When Everyday; Día Nacional de la Pupusa (second Sunday of November)

    Decree 655 of 2005 made pupusas the national dish and created a national day. November is the peak of the maize harvest.

    Key ingredients Maize (or rice) masa, cheese, chicharrón, beans, loroco

    How it is eaten By hand, with curtido (pickled cabbage) and tomato sauce

    Protected National dish by decree (2005)

  • 🇧🇷 Arroz e feijão · Rice and beans

    Where Brazil, national

    When Daily lunch

    The base of the Brazilian lunch. Black beans are preferred in RJ, RS and SC.

    Key ingredients White rice, beans (carioca or black)

    How it is eaten Beans ladled over rice

  • 🇬🇧 Jerk chicken with rice and peas

    Where England (British Caribbean; London, Birmingham, Bristol)

    When Sunday dinner, Notting Hill Carnival (August bank holiday), Christmas

    Jamaican jerk has Maroon and Taíno roots; the Windrush generation, named after HMT Empire Windrush (arrived June 1948), made rice and peas a Sunday staple of British Caribbean homes.

    Key ingredients Chicken, allspice (pimento), Scotch bonnet, thyme; rice cooked with kidney beans and coconut milk

    How it is eaten With coleslaw, fried plantain, hot pepper sauce

  • 🇪🇸 Gallo pinto (Nicaragua) · Rice and beans

    Where Nicaragua

    When Daily

    A mestizo dish of Indigenous beans and Spanish-introduced rice. Nicaragua and Costa Rica both claim it.

    Key ingredients Rice, red beans, onion, oil

    How it is eaten With eggs, cheese, plantain, tortilla

  • 🇧🇷 Prato feito (PF) · Set lunch plate

    Where Brazil, national

    When Weekday lunch

    Everyday lunch of rice and beans, salad and a protein.

    Key ingredients Rice, beans, steak or chicken, salad, fries, egg

    How it is eaten One plate, at a lunch counter

  • 🇬🇧 Red beans and rice

    Where USA (New Orleans)

    When Mondays (washday)

    Cooked slowly on Monday washday from Sunday's ham bone; Louis Armstrong signed letters 'Red beans and ricely yours'.

    Key ingredients Red kidney beans, ham bone, andouille, rice

    How it is eaten Over rice with cornbread

  • 🇪🇸 Gallo pinto (Costa Rica) · Rice and beans

    Where Costa Rica

    When Daily

    A shared and disputed dish with Nicaragua, with regional bean choices.

    Key ingredients Rice, black beans (Central Valley) or red beans (Guanacaste), onion, sweet pepper, coriander, Salsa Lizano

    How it is eaten With eggs, natilla (sour cream), tortilla, plantain

Fish and potatoes

  • 🇳🇱 Haringsalade · Herring salad

    Where NL – national; Limburg carnival

    When End of carnival

    Marks the end of carnival in Limburg.

    Key ingredients Herring, beetroot, potato, apple, mayonnaise

    How it is eaten Cold

  • 🇩🇪 Pannfisch · Pan-fried fish with mustard sauce

    Where DE – Hamburg

    When Year-round

    Once a leftover dish of the previous day's fish and potatoes, now a Hamburg classic made with fresh fish.

    Key ingredients Leftover white fish, fried potatoes, mustard sauce

    How it is eaten Fish on fried potatoes, covered with mustard sauce

  • 🇪🇸 Bacalao a la vizcaína · Biscay-style salt cod

    Where Mexico (Spanish heritage)

    When Christmas Eve, New Year

    Salt cod reached Mexico through the port of Veracruz.

    Key ingredients Salt cod, tomato, olives, capers, güero chiles, potatoes

    How it is eaten Hot, or in bolillo rolls the next day

  • 🇮🇹 Brandacujùn · Stockfish and potato cream

    Where Italy / Liguria (Ponente)

    When All year, Fridays

    Stockfish and potatoes worked to a creamy texture.

    Key ingredients Stockfish, potatoes, olive oil

    How it is eaten Warm, with fresh olive oil

  • 🇧🇷 Bolinho de bacalhau · Salt-cod fritter

    Where Brazil, SE (Rio)

    When Bars; Holy Week

    Portuguese heritage, mainly found in Rio.

    Key ingredients Salt cod, potato, parsley, egg

    How it is eaten With lemon, at the bar

  • 🇬🇧 Fish and chips · Chippy tea

    Where England (nationwide; also Scotland, Wales, NI, Ireland)

    When Friday, seaside

    Fried fish came with Sephardic Jewish immigrants; the first combined shops are claimed by Joseph Malin in London (1860s) and John Lees in Mossley, Lancashire (1863). Contested. Fish and chips stayed off the ration in WWII.

    Key ingredients Cod or haddock, batter, potatoes, beef dripping or oil

    How it is eaten Salt, malt vinegar, mushy peas, tartare sauce; wrapped in paper

  • 🇪🇸 Viudo de pescado · "Widower" fish stew

    Where Colombia: Magdalena river

    When Semana Santa

    Lenten fish dish.

    Key ingredients River fish, cassava, plantain, potato, hogao

    How it is eaten In a big pot, shared

  • 🇧🇷 Bacalhoada · Baked salt cod

    Where Brazil, national (Portuguese heritage)

    When Good Friday, Christmas

    Portuguese salt-cod tradition kept for Holy Week and Christmas.

    Key ingredients Salt cod, potatoes, onion, olives, olive oil

    How it is eaten From the dish, with rice

Lentils or chickpeas

  • 🇩🇪 Linsen mit Spätzle und Saitenwürstle · Lentils with Spätzle and sausages

    Where DE – Swabia

    When Year-round, weekdays

    The Swabian Saturday or weekday meal; Alb lentils were revived and are on the Slow Food Ark.

    Key ingredients Lentils (Alb-Leisa), Spätzle, Saitenwürstle (Frankfurter-type sausages), vinegar

    How it is eaten Sweet-sour lentils with a splash of vinegar

  • 🇪🇸 Fainá · Chickpea flatbread

    Where Argentina, Uruguay

    When With pizza

    From Genoese farinata. Served on pizza ("a caballo") in Argentina.

    Key ingredients Chickpea flour, water, oil

    How it is eaten A wedge on top of pizza

  • 🇮🇹 Farinata (fainâ) · Chickpea flatbread

    Where Italy / Liguria

    When All year

    A thin chickpea-flour, water and oil flatbread baked in a wood oven; called fainâ in Genoa. A Ligurian traditional product, with a Tuscan twin, cecina.

    Key ingredients Chickpea flour, water, olive oil

    How it is eaten Hot from a wood oven, black pepper

  • 🇧🇷 Lentilha de Ano Novo · New Year lentils

    Where Brazil, national

    When Réveillon, 31 December

    Lentils replace rice and beans at New Year.

    Key ingredients Lentils

    How it is eaten Eaten after midnight

  • 🇬🇧 Mulligatawny soup

    Where England (Anglo-Indian)

    When Winter, Victorian dinners

    From Tamil 'milagu thanni' (pepper water), adapted by the British in colonial Madras and brought home.

    Key ingredients Lentils or meat, curry spices, apple, rice

    How it is eaten In bowls, with rice

  • 🇩🇪 Linseneintopf · Lentil stew

    Where DE – all

    When Autumn and winter

    A weekday one-pot, finished with a splash of vinegar.

    Key ingredients Lentils, potatoes, carrots, bacon, vinegar

    How it is eaten With Mettwurst or Wiener sausages

  • 🇪🇸 Puchero andaluz · Andalusian stew

    Where Andalucía

    When Christmas and festive meals; winter

    A beef and vegetable stew served as a starter at festive meals. Its broth is a Christmas first course.

    Key ingredients Chickpeas, beef, chicken, pork, vegetables

    How it is eaten Broth first, then meats and chickpeas

  • 🇮🇹 Cotechino e lenticchie · Cotechino sausage with lentils

    Where Italy / Emilia-Romagna (Modena); all Italy

    When Capodanno (31 Dec–1 Jan)

    Lentils look like coins and so stand for money. Zampone came first, cotechino later. The pairing is strongest in the north.

    Key ingredients Cotechino, lentils

    How it is eaten At midnight or New Year lunch; more lentils, more money

    Protected Cotechino Modena IGP

Apples that need using up

  • 🇳🇱 Hete bliksem · "Hot lightning" (potato and apple)

    Where NL – national

    When Autumn/winter

    Called "hemel en aarde" in the east and "pronkjewail" or "stamppot zoete appeltjes" in the north.

    Key ingredients Potatoes, sweet and sour apples, bacon or blood sausage, stroop

    How it is eaten Hot, sometimes with syrup

  • 🇩🇪 Matjes nach Hausfrauenart · Matjes herring housewife-style

    Where DE – North, Rhineland

    When June (new Matjes), summer

    Matjes are young herring caught before spawning and mildly salted; the new season's Matjes are celebrated in June. Glückstädter Matjes is a PGI.

    Key ingredients Young salted herring, sour cream, apple, onion, gherkin

    How it is eaten With boiled new potatoes or jacket potatoes

    Protected Glückstädter Matjes PGI (2015)

  • 🇪🇸 Tacos al pastor · Shepherd-style spit-roast pork tacos

    Where Mexico: CDMX, Puebla

    When Everyday, gatherings

    Comes from the shawarma spit brought by Lebanese and other Middle Eastern migrants, with pork in place of lamb. The dates (1930s or 1960s) and inventors are disputed.

    Key ingredients Pork marinated with achiote and dried chiles, pineapple, corn tortilla

    How it is eaten Small tacos with onion, coriander, pineapple and salsa

  • 🇮🇹 Fritto misto alla piemontese · Piedmontese mixed fry

    Where Italy / Piemonte

    When Feasts

    Sweet and savoury pieces, breaded and fried together.

    Key ingredients Meats, offal, sweet semolina, apples, amaretti

    How it is eaten Hot, in one big platter

  • 🇧🇷 Maçã do amor · Candy apple

    Where Brazil, national

    When Festa Junina

    A June fairground sweet.

    Key ingredients Apple, red sugar glaze

    How it is eaten On a stick

  • 🇬🇧 Roast pork with crackling and apple sauce

    Where England

    When Sunday lunch

    Scored, salted skin crisps into 'crackling'; sharp apple sauce has been paired with pork since at least the 18th century.

    Key ingredients Pork loin or belly with skin, salt, Bramley apples

    How it is eaten Sliced with crackling, apple sauce, sage and onion stuffing

    Protected Ingredient protected: Gloucestershire Old Spots pork TSG

  • 🇳🇱 Vijfschaft · Five-part mash

    Where NL – national

    When Winter

    A workers' stamppot of potato, carrot, onion, apple and brown beans; 'schaft' is an old word for a workers' meal break.

    Key ingredients Potato, onion, apple, carrot, brown beans

    How it is eaten Hot

  • 🇩🇪 Heringssalat / Heringsstipp · Herring salad

    Where DE – Rhineland, North

    When Heiligabend and Silvester in many families

    Herring salad is a common cold dish on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve in many families; Christmas Eve was once the last day of the Advent fast, when fish replaced meat.

    Key ingredients Herring, apple, onion, gherkin, beetroot or cream sauce

    How it is eaten With jacket potatoes or dark bread
