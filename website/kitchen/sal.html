What Sal does

Cook with what's left

Tonight's dinner from what is in the fridge, the food that goes off first on top. When no dish fits, a new one of his own, marked as new.

🗓️

Plan my week

The coming dinners around your evenings, your allergies and diets, your budget and what needs using up, with a kitchen to travel to now and then.

The list from the week plan, minus what is already in the pantry, grouped the way your shop is laid out.

A menu for the number at the table and their allergies, with a timeline for the day, so the cook sits down too.

On your own Claude

Connect Sal to your Claude app: a plugin and a folder, set up in a few steps, with a paid Claude plan. Or use a Claude API key: then you pay Anthropic for what you use. Nothing goes through Busy.

What Sal never does

Sal gives no medical or diet advice and makes no health claims: for that, a dietitian or a doctor is the right person. A recipe he creates or adapts is marked new and not yet cooked by us. He lists the allergens and never calls a dish safe for an allergy: always check the labels of what you buy. He never shops, orders or pays.

Questions

Is Sal a person?

No. Sal is an AI chef that runs on Claude, made by Anthropic, on your own Claude account. He works from Busy Kitchen's dishes and from your own recipes, pantry and week plan.

What does he cost?

Sal comes in Busy Kitchen with Sal: $1.49 once on itch.io. He runs on your own Claude: with the Claude app and a paid Claude plan, or with a Claude API key, when you pay Anthropic for what you use.

Are Sal's own recipes tested?

No. A recipe Sal creates or adapts is new: it is marked as Sal's new recipe, not yet cooked by us. Busy's house recipes are checked against written rules, and are also not yet cooked by us.

Does Sal give diet or medical advice?

No. Sal cooks: he can leave gluten or dairy out of the ingredients, and he lists the allergens. Managing an allergy, an illness or a diet is for a dietitian or a doctor.

Does Sal order the shopping?

No. Sal prepares the week plan and the shopping list. Each waits for your yes, and you do the shopping.

Where are my recipes?

In your own kitchen folder on your computer. What Claude reads goes to Anthropic under your own account, never to us.

Is Sal in the free download?

No. The free Busy Kitchen has the six kitchens and every tool, and no AI. Sal comes in his own download when you buy him, with Busy Kitchen included.
