Busy Kitchen · the six kitchens

The food calendar of six kitchens

The feast days and seasons of six kitchens, and what is on the table, month by month.

🍳 Open Busy Kitchen free

January

  • 🇳🇱 1 January Stamppot snijbonen in some regions; kniepertjes in the Northeast; Flemish children read New Year's letters Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 6 January, Driekoningen Driekoningentaart with a bean or figurine (Flanders, growing in West-Vlaanderen); derthienkoeck at the Groot Begijnhof in Leuven Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇩🇪 1 Jan, Neujahr Neujahrsbrezel (Pfalz, Baden, Swabia), pork for luck, herring after the night before, marzipan pigs German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 6 Jan, Dreikönigstag Dreikönigskuchen (CH): revived from 1952 by bread researcher Max Währen, with a plastic king hidden inside German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 Fasching, Fastnacht, Karneval (Jan–Feb/Mar) Krapfen, Berliner, Kreppel, Kräppel; Fasnetsküchle and Schmalzgebäck (Swabia); Mutzen (Rhineland); Fasnachtschüechli, Schenkeli, Basler Mehlsuppe and Zwiebelwähe (Basel, after the Morgestraich); Funkenküchle (Vorarlberg, Funkensonntag) German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇪🇸 5–6 Jan Roscón de Reyes, eaten on the afternoon of 5 January during the Cabalgata or on 6 January, with a hidden figurine; Valencian casca; Asturian puff-pastry version Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 6 Jan Día de Reyes (Mexico and others) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 1 Jan (and 31 Dec night) Cotechino or zampone with lentils (north, Emilia, Lombardy); lentil soup (Tuscany); sciusci fried rings on 31 Dec (Molise) Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 6 Jan Ciciu 'd capdan bread dolls (Cuneo); the end of the Christmas sweets season Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 17 Jan Start of pig-slaughter dishes such as cassoeula Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 29 Jan Torcolo di San Costanzo Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 Jan–Feb/Mar Chiacchiere under many names: bugie (Piedmont), frappe (Lazio), cenci (Tuscany), crostoli (Trentino), sfrappole (Emilia), galani (Venice/Verona), lattughe (Mantua). Also castagnole, fritole veneziane, cicerchiata (Abruzzo, Marche), graffe (Naples), tortelli (Milan), smacafam (Trentino), rafanata (Basilicata) Italian food
  • 🇧🇷 6 January (Reis) Folia de Reis (no food source fetched) Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 1 January Hogmanay into New Year: black bun, shortbread, steak pie on New Year's Day (Scotland) British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 6 January Twelfth Night (historic Twelfth cake, ancestor of the Christmas cake) British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 25 January Burns Night (Scotland and diaspora): haggis, neeps and tatties, cranachan British, Irish and American food

February

  • 🇳🇱 2 February, Lichtmis Pancakes (Belgium) Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Carnival (Feb/Mar) Nonnevotten (Limburg, sold from about a month before until Ash Wednesday); zoervleis with fries; haringsalade at the end Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇪🇸 Feb (varies) Panchón de Carnaval (Asturias), frixuelos, filloas, flores Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 2 Feb Candelaria (Mexico) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Early Feb Día Nacional de la Pachamanca (Peru, first Sunday of Feb per Agraria) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Feb–Mar Carnaval Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇧🇷 Carnaval (February/March) Barreado was historically a Carnaval dish in coastal Paraná Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 1 February St Brigid's Day (Ireland, public holiday since 2023): bread, butter, barmbrack-type breads British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 February (first Sunday or so) — British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 Shrove Tuesday (Feb/Mar) Pancake Day: thin pancakes with lemon and sugar; Olney pancake race; crempog (Wales) British, Irish and American food

March

  • 🇳🇱 Spring (Mar–Jun) Raapstelen; Poperingse hopscheuten; white asparagus Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇪🇸 15–19 Mar Buñuelos de calabaza with chocolate Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 19 Mar Crema catalana, also called "crema de Sant Josep" Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Lent / Semana Santa (Mar–Apr) Cuaresma and Holy Week Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 19 Mar Zeppole (Campania), sfince (Sicily), bignè (Rome), frittelle di riso (Tuscany, Umbria), raviole (Emilia), calzone di San Giuseppe (Molise), tripoline with anchovies at Salento tavole, pasta con le sarde (Sicily) Italian food
  • 🇬🇧 1 March St David's Day (Wales): cawl, Welsh cakes, bara brith, leeks British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 17 March St Patrick's Day (Ireland, Northern Ireland): Irish stew, bacon and cabbage, soda bread British, Irish and American food

April

  • 🇳🇱 27 April, Koningsdag Oranje tompouce Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇩🇪 mid-Apr to 24 Jun Spargelzeit, ending on Johannistag ("Spargelsilvester") German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇪🇸 14 Apr Día Nacional del Vori Vori (Paraguay, Ley 7703) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 25 Apr Risi e bisi Italian food

May

  • 🇳🇱 30 May Zeeuwse Bolusdag (since 2015) Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇩🇪 May–Jun Strawberries, rhubarb cake, new Matjes (June), elderflower syrup German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇪🇸 15 May Rosquillas tontas y listas, barquillos, gallinejas and entresijos Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 25 May Revolución de Mayo (Argentina) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 May–Jun Corpus Christi (Cusco) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 1 May Virtù teramane (Teramo); Riccia's calzone also on 1 May Italian food
  • 🇧🇷 Last Sunday of May n/a Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 5 May (Cinco de Mayo) — British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 Late May — British, Irish and American food

June

  • 🇪🇸 23 Jun Coca de Sant Joan Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 24 Jun Inti Raymi (Cusco) / Fiesta de San Juan (Peruvian Amazon) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇧🇷 June Festa Junina on 13 (Santo Antônio), 24 (São João) and 29 June (São Pedro). The Northeast celebrates the end of the rains. Foods: pamonha, curau, canjica/mungunzá, milho cozido, pipoca, paçoca, pé-de-moleque, cuscuz, bolo de milho, pinhão in the South, quentão (alcoholic, excluded), Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 June Strawberries and cream (Wimbledon), Eton mess, elderflower cordial British, Irish and American food

July

  • 🇳🇱 1 July, Keti Koti Surinamese food: bojo, telo met pindasaus, pom Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇪🇸 9 Jul Independence Day (Argentina) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 28 Jul Fiestas Patrias (Peru) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇬🇧 4 July — British, Irish and American food

August

  • 🇳🇱 August Mastellen at the Gentse Patersholfeesten; hoofdvlees at the Gentse Feesten Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇩🇪 Aug–Sep Zwetschgenkuchen and Datschi, Zwetschgenknödel; chanterelles; Marillenknödel (Wachau, July) German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇪🇸 Aug–Sep Chile en nogada season (Puebla) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 14–15 Aug Picnics and roasts; Montecilfone's drocchie pasta feast on 14 Aug (Molise). No national Ferragosto dish was confirmed Italian food
  • 🇬🇧 Late August Ould Lammas Fair, Ballycastle: yellowman and dulse British, Irish and American food

September

  • 🇩🇪 late Sep–early Oct Oktoberfest season: Wiesn-Hendl, Schweinshaxe, Ochsenbraten, Steckerlfisch, Brezn, Obatzda, Weißwurst, gebrannte Mandeln, Lebkuchenherzen German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 Sep–Oct Zwiebelkuchen (with new wine, Federweißer, not catalogued), harvest festivals, Kirchweih and Kirta pastries, game, pumpkin, Vermicelles (CH), carp season starts ("months with an R") German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇪🇸 15–16 Sep Fiestas patrias (Mexico) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 18–19 Sep Fiestas Patrias (Chile) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 Sep–Nov Bagna càuda in the wine-harvest season; white truffle on tajarin; chestnuts and castagnaccio; hundreds of local sagre (summer–autumn food festivals) Italian food
  • 🇧🇷 September Caruru for Cosme and Damião (27 September) in Bahia Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 September / Harvest Harvest festival suppers; blackberrying; apple crumble British, Irish and American food

October

  • 🇳🇱 3 October, Leidens Ontzet Hutspot, herring and white bread Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇪🇸 Oct Señor de los Milagros (Lima) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 31 Oct – 2 Nov Día de Muertos / Hanal Pixán (Mexico); Día de Difuntos (Ecuador, 2 Nov); Todos los Santos (Guatemala, 1 Nov) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇧🇷 2nd Sunday of October Círio de Nazaré in Belém. The family lunch serves maniçoba (cooking starts the Monday before), pato no tucupi and tacacá, Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 31 October Halloween (Ireland's Samhain roots): barmbrack with a ring, colcannon with charms; toffee apples British, Irish and American food

November

  • 🇳🇱 11 November, Sint-Maarten Sweets for children (Utrecht, Denderstreek and Ieper area in Flanders) Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇩🇪 Nov–Feb Grünkohl season "nach dem ersten Frost" and Kohlfahrten (Bremen, Oldenburg, East Frisia); Schlachtfest; Raclette and Fondue German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 11 Nov, Martinstag Martinsgans or Martinigansl (DE, AT), Weckmann and Stutenkerl at lantern parades German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇪🇸 1 Nov Huesos de santo, buñuelos de viento, panellets, gachas, arrope Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 2nd Sunday of Nov Día Nacional de la Pupusa (El Salvador, Decree 655 of 2005) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 30 Nov Día Nacional del Mate (Argentina) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 1–2 Nov Fave dei morti (centre and north-east); pan dei morti (Milan, Brianza); ossa dei morti (Sicily, also attributed elsewhere); frutta di Martorana (Palermo); grano cotto (Matera); pabassinas (Sardinia) Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 11 Nov Pizza scema with hidden seeds (Molise) Italian food
  • 🇧🇷 1 November n/a Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇧🇷 11 November n/a Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 5 November Bonfire Night: parkin, bonfire (treacle) toffee, toffee apples, jacket potatoes, sausages, soup British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 Fourth Thursday November — British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 30 November St Andrew's Day (Scotland) British, Irish and American food

December

  • 🇳🇱 5 December (NL) / 6 December (BE) Speculaas, taaitaai, pepernoten, kruidnoten, borstplaat, chocoladeletter, banketletter, marsepein; klaaskoeken in Flanders Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 31 December, Oud en Nieuw Oliebollen, appelbeignets, appelflappen, ananasbeignets Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇩🇪 6 Dec, Nikolaus Stutenkerl and Weckmann (DE), Grittibänz (CH), nuts, mandarins, Lebkuchen German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 24 Dec, Heiligabend Kartoffelsalat mit Würstchen (most common in DE); carp (Austria, Bavaria, the East); fondue chinoise in Switzerland German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 25–26 Dec Goose, duck, Rouladen, game, Klöße, red cabbage; Christstollen with coffee (c for poultry dominance) German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 31 Dec, Silvester Raclette or Fondue, Berliner (with a mustard-filled joke one), carp, herring salad, Bleigießen (custom, not food) German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇪🇸 21 Dec Talo con txistorra Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 24 Dec Seafood, besugo, sopa de almendras, cardo, caracoles (Cantabria), bacalao con coliflor (Galicia), besugo en salsa (La Rioja) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 25 Dec Escudella i carn d'olla, puchero, putxero de Nadal, roast lamb, suckling pig, capon, caldo con pelotas Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 26 Dec Canelons Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 31 Dec Twelve grapes at the midnight chimes, recorded in the press by 1882 and popularised by the 1909 Alicante grape surplus Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 All December Turrón, polvorones, mantecados, mazapán, marquesitas, neules, cordiales, guirlache, alfajores Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 16–24 Dec Novena (Colombia), posadas (Mexico) Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 24–25 Dec Nochebuena and Navidad Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 13 Dec Panonta (Molise); occhi di Santa Lucia (Bari). Sicilian arancine and cuccìa on this day: Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 24 Dec Meatless feast: capitone and baccalà (Naples, Lazio, Calabria), spaghetti alle vongole, cappon magro (Genoa), bigoli in salsa (Veneto), tortelli di zucca (Mantua), pasta with breadcrumbs and anchovies (south), culurgiones de casu (Sardinia), zengarielle (Molise) Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 25–26 Dec Tortellini or cappelletti in broth, agnolotti, natalini, roast capon, bollito; panettone, pandoro, panforte, struffoli, cartellate, pandolce, gubana, zelten Italian food
  • 🇧🇷 24–25 December Ceia de Natal: peru or chester, tender, farofa, salpicão, maionese, bacalhoada, rabanada, panetone, fruits; leitão in Minas Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇧🇷 31 December Lentils at the Réveillon Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 December Mince pies, Christmas cake, spiced beef (Cork) British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 25 December Christmas dinner (turkey, ham in Ireland) British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 26 December Boxing Day (St Stephen's Day in Ireland): cold cuts, bubble and squeak, turkey curry, pork pie British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 31 December Hogmanay British, Irish and American food

Moving feasts and seasons

  • 🇳🇱 Halfvasten (mid-Lent) Mastellen in Gent Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Wednesday before Easter Herring handed out in Niekerk, Oldekerk and Faan (Groningen), recorded since 1476 Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Pasen Paasstol, chocolate eggs Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Herring season Hollandse Nieuwe: catch 1 May–31 Aug per the TSG file; first barrel and Vlaggetjesdag in Scheveningen (since 1781) Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Summer Schaafijs; aardbeien met beschuit Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Kermis (fair season) Poffertjes, oliebollen, smoutebollen, kermiskoek; lacquemants at Antwerp's Sinksenfoor Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Autumn Game, hazenpeper Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Kerst Kerststol at brunch, kerstkransjes, rollade, gourmetten, stoofperen, tulband; cougnou in Belgium Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇳🇱 Winter on the ice Snert, chocolademelk, anijsmelk, koek-en-zopie Dutch and Flemish food
  • 🇩🇪 Aschermittwoch Heringsessen (herring dinner) German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 Lent Fasting foods: Brezel, Maultaschen (legend), fish, Spinat German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 Gründonnerstag Frankfurter Grüne Soße, spinach with eggs, green herb dishes German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 Karfreitag Fish German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 Ostern Lamb roast; Osterlamm cake; Osterzopf and Osterbrot; dyed eggs; Kärntner Osterjause and Fleischweihe (Reindling, ham, Kren, eggs); Steirische Osterpinze; Osterfladen (Basel) German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 Summer Rote Grütze, berries; Birnen, Bohnen und Speck (Hamburg, late summer); grilling with Rostbratwurst; Apfelschorle German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇩🇪 Advent Plätzchen baking; Stollen, Lebkuchen, Printen, Spekulatius, Springerle; Christmas markets (Striezelmarkt Dresden, Christkindlesmarkt Nürnberg); Kinderpunsch German, Austrian and Swiss food
  • 🇪🇸 Lent, Fridays Potaje de vigilia, salt cod (tortitas, empanada de bacalao con pasas), sopa de ajo con bacalao Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Holy Week Torrijas, pestiños, flores/floretas, crespillos, buñuelos, chochos de Lorca, coca de llanda, arnadí, panades, truchas de batata Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Good Friday dawn Sopa de ajo eaten in the procession break Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Easter Sunday Laconada (Galicia), cocido montañés, cordero al chilindrón Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Easter Monday / Sunday Mona de Pascua, given by godparents Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Monday after Easter week Hornazo eaten by the river Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Summer Gazpacho, salmorejo, ajoblanco, horchata with fartons Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Winter to spring Calçots with romesco Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Autumn Roasted chestnuts, panellets, moniatos Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇪🇸 Late autumn to winter Fresh pork, botillo feasts, sausages Spanish and Latin American food
  • 🇮🇹 Lent, Fridays Baccalà and stockfish, meatless soups; Molise's baccalà ammullecate for Lent and Advent Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 Holy Week, Easter, Pasquetta Lamb (abbacchio, buglione); pastiera and casatiello (Campania); pizza or crescia di Pasqua (Umbria, Marche, Lazio, Abruzzo); torta pasqualina; colomba everywhere; cassata and pecorella (Sicily); agnello di pasta di mandorle (Lecce); pardulas (Sardinia); pigna (Molise, south Lazio); ciaramicola (Perugia); cudduraci on Easter Monday (Calabria); ciambella romagnola; gubana Italian food
  • 🇮🇹 Summer Pappa al pomodoro, panzanella, frisella, granita, sarde in saor for Venice's Redentore, almond milk Italian food
  • 🇧🇷 Lent and Semana Santa Torta capixaba, moqueca and bacalhoada on Good Friday Bahian fish and coconut dishes Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇧🇷 Páscoa Chocolate eggs; bacalhau Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇧🇷 Pentecost to Trinity Sunday Festa do Divino (no food source fetched) Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇧🇷 Seasonal produce Pequi; pinhão (autumn to winter); tainha; açaí harvest Portuguese and Brazilian food
  • 🇬🇧 Lent Fridays Fish British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 Mothering Sunday (Lent) Simnel cake (historically) British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 Good Friday / Easter Hot cross buns; Easter Sunday roast lamb; simnel cake; chocolate eggs British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 Spring Asparagus (Evesham), Jersey Royals, forced rhubarb ends British, Irish and American food
  • 🇬🇧 Last Sunday before Advent Stir-up Sunday: Christmas puddings made at home British, Irish and American food
