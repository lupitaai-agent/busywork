How to start

  1. Unzip it. Double-click the zip file. Move the Busy folder somewhere you’ll find it again, like Documents.
  2. Open index.html. Double-click it. It opens in your browser. Tip: bookmark it, so it’s one click away.
  3. Pick your business folder. Make a new folder like “My Business”. Put it inside Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive if you want backups and syncing.
  4. Tell us your business name. Plus your logo and bank details if you like. Then make your first invoice.
