Questions, answered

What is Olaf?

Olaf is our AI marketing expert for small businesses. It works out where your business stands, makes a 90-day plan, checks in every week and does the work each step needs: posts, review requests, your newsletter, your local listing. It is built on proven marketing science, runs in your browser on your own Claude key, and costs €149 once. Get on the list.

What is the difference between the free Marketing Studio and Olaf?

The Studio gives you the playbooks and you do the work, with AI help on each task if you add your own Claude key. Olaf is our AI marketing expert, right inside Busywork: it works out where your business stands, makes a 90-day plan, checks in with you every week, and does the work each step needs. Both use the same marketing science. More about Olaf.

Do I need Olaf to use the tools?

No. Every tool, the Marketing Studio included, is free and complete on its own. Olaf is for owners who would rather be guided than figure out their marketing alone.

Is it really free?

Yes. The website shows a few ads, and that keeps the tools free. There are no ads inside your invoices or documents, ever.

Where is my data stored?

In a folder on your computer that you choose. Each tool saves plain files there. We have no copy and no way to see it. If you use the AI features, what you ask about goes from your browser to Anthropic with your own key, and nowhere else. See the privacy page.

Can I use Google Drive?

Yes. Install Google Drive for desktop and pick a folder inside it. Drive backs it up and keeps it in sync across your computers.

Which browsers work?

Chrome and Edge on Mac and Windows can use a folder. Safari and Firefox can still try everything, saving inside the browser.

Can I work on it with my team?

Share the folder through Google Drive and everyone sees the same clients and invoices. Team Mode is on the workbench.

Do the invoices meet the rules?

They include what EU invoices need: numbers that count up, dates, both parties, VAT per rate and your VAT number. Check with your accountant for anything special.
