Questions, answered

What is Busy?

Busy makes free, private tools for work and life, in suites: Busy Business for small businesses, Busy Games for a daily brain break, and soon Busy Growth for your career. Everything runs in your browser and stays in your own folder.

Is it really free?

Yes. The tools in every suite are free, with no account, no trial and no subscription. If you want an AI expert, you pay once: Olaf for business, Ivy for your career (coming soon).

Where is my data?

In a folder you choose on your own computer, as plain files. We have no copy and no way to see it. The games keep your streak in your browser.

Which suite is for me?

Run a business or work freelance? Start with Busy Business. Want a five-minute brain break? Busy Games. Want to learn, grow and land a better job? Busy Growth is coming soon. Drowning in household paperwork? Busy Home is on its way.
