Questions, answered

What is Busy?

Busy makes private tools for work and life, and games, in suites: Busy Business for small businesses, Busy Home for household paperwork, Busy Games for a break, and Busy Growth for your career. The tools are free. Each suite also comes as a paid app with an expert inside: Olaf, your brand consultant; Ivy, your career coach; Juno, your household assistant.

Is it really free?

Yes. The tools in every suite are free, with no account, no trial and no subscription, and they stay free. They have no AI in them.

What do I pay for?

The same app with its expert inside: Olaf for your brand, Ivy for your growth, Juno for your home. The expert prepares the work and you say yes. You pay once and keep it, and updates are free. The full Busy Street games are sold the same way.

How does an expert run?

On the Claude you already have. An expert is Claude, made by Anthropic: you add it to your own Claude app in three steps, with no key and no second bill. A Claude API key works too, if you prefer one.

Why do the buy buttons go to itch.io?

itch.io takes the payment and gives you the download, so we never see your card. Each button opens that product's own page in a new tab.

Where is my data?

In a folder you choose on your own computer, as plain files. We have no copy and no way to see it. With an expert, what you ask goes from your computer to Anthropic, never to us. The games keep your streak in your browser.

Which languages?

English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese: this site, the apps, the experts and both Busy Street games.

Which suite is for me?

Run a business or work freelance? Start with Busy Business. Want a five-minute brain break? Busy Games. Want to learn, grow and land a better job? Start with Busy Growth. Drowning in household paperwork? Busy Home.
