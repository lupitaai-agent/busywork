Connect your expert to Claude

Olaf, Ivy, Juno, Wren and Sal think with Claude, the AI made by Anthropic. There are two ways to connect them. Both keep your work on your own computer, and nothing goes to us.

Works on your own Claude: the Claude app with a paid plan, or a Claude API key. New to Claude? The free app is complete on its own.

Just bought on itch.io? Download your app from its itch.io page and open it. Press Connect in the left rail: the app shows these same steps.

The easy way: the Claude app

For anyone with the Claude app and a paid Claude plan. The expert works inside your own Claude: if you already pay for Claude, the expert's price is all you pay.

  1. Add the expert to your Claude
    In Busy, press Connect and save the plugin file. In Claude, open Cowork, choose Customize, then Plugins, and upload the file.
  2. Show Claude your Busy folder
    When Claude asks which folder to work in, choose the folder Busy saves in.
  3. Say hello
    Busy opens Claude with the first sentence typed in. Press Enter.

From then on the expert prepares the work in Claude, and it shows in Busy under “Needs your yes”. Nothing changes in your records until you say yes.

Or with your own API key

For anyone who prefers to pay as they go. You pay Anthropic directly for what you use, and your key never leaves your browser except to go to Anthropic. Setting it up takes about five minutes, once.

You need

  • An email address
  • A payment card
  • Five minutes

The steps

  1. Make a Claude Console account
    Go to console.anthropic.com and sign up with your email or Google account. Confirm your email. If it asks what you will build, “personal use” is fine.
  2. Add a little credit
    Open Settings → Billing (it may be called Plans & Billing), add a card and buy credit. The smallest amount, about $5, is enough to start. Leave auto-reload off if you never want to pay more than you put in. Under Settings → Limits you can also set a monthly spend limit.
  3. Create your API key
    Open Settings → API keys and click Create key. Name it something like “Busy Olaf” or “Busy Ivy”. Copy it straight away: it starts with sk-ant- and is shown only once. Lost it? Delete it and make a new one.
  4. Paste it into your expert
    The first time you start one, a guide asks for the key, tests it with a tiny request and saves it in that browser. You also set a monthly limit in Busy, and Busy stops sending requests when you reach it.

Questions

Does my Claude subscription cover this?

With the easy way, yes: the expert then works inside your own Claude app, within the plan you already pay for. An API key is billed separately by Anthropic, pay-as-you-go.

What does the expert see?

Busy writes a short briefing into a folder called Claude inside your Busy folder: where things stand today. Your Claude reads that, and writes its prepared work back into the same folder. It never changes your records: Busy does that, when you say yes.

Where is my key kept?

In the browser on your computer only: never in your business or growth folder, never on a Busy server (there is none). It is sent only to Anthropic, with each request.

What if someone gets my key?

Delete it in the console under Settings → API keys, make a new one and paste that in. The old one stops working at once.

Can I use it on two computers?

Yes. Paste the same key on each, or make one key per computer so you can switch one off on its own.

How do I see what I spent?

Busy shows an estimate for this month in AI settings. The exact amount is in the console under Usage and Billing.
