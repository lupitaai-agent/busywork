About Busy

Five free apps for your brand, your growth, your home, the home you let and your kitchen. The boring part made easy, or done for you by an expert. Everything stays in your own folder.

What it is
Busy makes free, private tools for work and life, in suites: Busy Business (thirteen tools for small businesses and freelancers to grow and run the business: a marketing studio with a brand profile and a year plan, a brand kit, projects, invoices and quotes, time, receipts, money, clients, documents, subscriptions, trips and stock), Busy Growth (free tools to grow your career: a library, skills, wins, a job tracker, a CV and LinkedIn builder and more), Busy Home (free tools for household paperwork: documents with expiry reminders, contracts with the last day to cancel, upkeep, a home inventory, letters, moving and a Just in Case binder), Busy Rentals (free rental management for people who let one home or a few: every date, certificate, deposit, rent payment and receipt, with the landlord's year for England, the Netherlands and Germany), Busy Kitchen (a free cultural kitchen: 1,886 dishes from the six kitchens of Busy's six languages, matched to what's in your fridge, with a recipe for every dish) and Busy Games (free daily brain games). Each suite is a free app for Mac and Windows, or runs in the browser, and keeps everything in the owner's own folder, with no account and no subscription. Each suite also comes as a paid app with an AI expert inside, bought once on itch.io: Olaf for business, Ivy for professional growth, Juno for household paperwork, Wren for letting a home, Sal for home cooking. Busy also makes the game Busy Street.
The suites
Busy Business: 13 free tools for small businesses and freelancers. Busy Growth: 9 free tools to grow your career. Busy Home: 8 free tools for household paperwork. Busy Rentals: 4 free tools for people who let one home or a few. Busy Kitchen: 1,886 dishes from six kitchens, matched to what's in your fridge. Busy Games: four free daily logic puzzles with 10,000 levels each.
Who it is for
People who would rather spend their time on what matters: small business owners and freelancers, anyone growing their career, households with paperwork to keep, people who let a home, home cooks, and anyone who likes a daily puzzle.
What it costs
Every suite's tools are free. A paid edition of each suite adds its AI expert, bought once on itch.io: Busy Business with Olaf $7.99, Busy Growth with Ivy $1.49, Busy Home with Juno $1.49, Busy Rentals with Wren $4.99, Busy Kitchen with Sal $1.49. The experts work on the owner's own Claude: the Claude app with a paid plan, or a Claude API key.
Where the data lives
In a folder the owner chooses on their own computer, as plain files. There is no account and no server, so nothing is sent to us. The games keep your streak in your browser.
What it runs on
Busy is desktop apps for Mac (macOS 13 or newer) and Windows (10 or 11), each with a demo that runs in the browser. The free Busy Kitchen opens in the browser. The games run in any modern browser.
How the marketing tools work
Busy Business's Marketing Studio and Olaf are built on proven marketing science: reach more buyers, be the business people think of in the moments they need you, and be easy to find and buy from.
Languages
English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. Invoices, quotes and letters can be made in any of them.
The name
Busy, free, private tools for work and life, at busy.com.ai. Called Busywork until October 2026. Not related to BUSY accounting software.

💼 Busy Business

  • 🧾 Invoices & Quotes: Make professional invoices and quotes in a minute. VAT, numbering, PDF, scan-to-pay QR code, reminders and recurring invoices. Free, private, no account.
  • 📇 Client Book: Keep every client in one friendly place: contact details, payment terms, rates, notes and invoice history. Import from Excel or Google Sheets. Free and private.
  • ⏱️ Time Tracker: Track hours with one button and turn them into an invoice in one click. Billable rates per client, rounding, a weekly timesheet. Free and private.
  • 📸 Receipts & Expenses: Snap or drop receipts and we file them in your folder, count the VAT you can claim back and make a spreadsheet for your accountant. Free and private.
  • 📈 Money Dashboard: See profit, VAT to set aside and cash flow at a glance, from your own invoices and receipts. Free, private, no account.
  • 🗓️ Project Planner: Plan client jobs, launches and events: milestones, a drag-and-drop timeline, who does what, risks and a weekly update. Free, private, no account.
  • 🗂️ Project Board: A playful kanban board: drag cards from to-do to done, add checklists and due dates, and track time on any card. Free and private.
  • 📝 Document Maker: Letters, payment reminders, proposals, price lists, project briefs and simple agreements, with names and addresses filled in and your logo on every PDF. Free.
  • 🎨 Brand Kit: Turn your logo and colour into a palette, a proper email signature for Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, and print-ready business cards with a QR code. Free.
  • 🔁 Subscriptions: Every monthly and yearly bill in one place: true monthly cost, renewal warnings, a 12-month timeline and one-click expense logging. Free and private.
  • 🚗 Trip Log: Log business trips in seconds, with return trips, frequent routes and a yearly total worth money at tax time. Export for your accountant. Free and private.
  • 📦 Stock Keeper: Tap plus and minus as stock comes and goes, get low-stock warnings, scan barcodes, see stock value, margins and a full history per item. Free and private.
  • 📣 Marketing Studio: Do your own marketing on proven science: a brand profile, a year plan, a content calendar and insights from your invoices. Free and private.
