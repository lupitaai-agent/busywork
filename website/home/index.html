Busy Home in short

What is Busy Home?

Busy Home is a free app for Mac and Windows for a household's paperwork: documents with their expiry dates, contracts with the last day to cancel, upkeep, an inventory, letters, a moving planner, a Just in Case binder and shared costs. Everything is saved in a folder on your own computer.

Who is it for?

Busy Home is for any household, from one person to a family with pets: whoever looks after the contracts, the insurance, the letters and the dates.

What does it cost?

Busy Home is free, with no account and no subscription. Juno, the AI household assistant, comes in the paid edition: Busy Home with Juno, $1.49 once on itch.io.

Is there AI in the free version?

No. The free Busy Home has no AI and sends nothing anywhere. Only Juno, in the paid edition, uses AI: Claude, on your own Claude account.

Where is my data?

Busy Home saves plain files in Documents/Busy/Home on your own computer, or in a folder you choose. Put it in Google Drive or Dropbox to share it with your partner. Nothing is sent to us.

Which languages?

Busy Home is in English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. Juno writes a letter in the language of the person who receives it.

Opening it the first time

The beta isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks first, once. Mac: open the downloaded file, drag Busy Home into Applications and open it. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Windows: run the downloaded file; if Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Can’t install apps? Use the browser version (zip) in Chrome or Edge.
