Free · Beta · Mac: macOS 13 or newer, Apple chip or Intel · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Opening it the first time

The beta isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks first, once. Mac: open the downloaded file, drag Busy Home into Applications and open it. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Windows: run the downloaded file; if Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Can’t install apps? Use the browser version (zip) in Chrome or Edge.
