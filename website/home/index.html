Busy Home 🏡

Free tools for the paperwork every household has: passports, policies, contracts, upkeep, the things you own and the letters you need to send. Everything in your own folder, no account, like the rest of Busy.

7 tools that work together

A contract knows its cancellation letter, the move knows your contracts, and the Just in Case binder knows where every document is.

🗂️

Paper Trail

Passports, policies, contracts and warranties in one place, searchable down to the words inside a PDF, with a nudge before anything expires.

🔁

Contracts & Renewals

Insurance, energy, phone, the gym: what each costs a month and the last day to cancel before it renews by itself.

🛠️

Home Upkeep

The boiler service, smoke alarms and gutters: when each is due, when it was done, by whom and for how much.

🛋️

Home Inventory

What you own, room by room, with photos, receipts and warranties. After a break-in, the claim pack is one click away.

✉️

Letters

Cancel, complain, claim, object or ask for your data: letters with your details, the company and the dates filled in.

🚚

Moving Planner

A checklist worked back from moving day, and everyone to tell, with your contracts already on the list.

📕

Just in Case

Everything your family would need if something happened to you: people to call, where things are, wishes. One PDF.

🔒

The most private paperwork you have

Your passports never leave your folder

IDs, insurance policies, contracts, the family's plans: this is the most sensitive paperwork a household has. Busy Home keeps all of it as plain files in a folder you choose. There is no Busy account and no Busy server, so there is nothing to hack and nothing to sell.
