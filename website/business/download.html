How to start

  1. Install it. On a Mac, open the downloaded file and drag Busy into Applications. On Windows, run the downloaded file.
  2. Open it the first time. The beta isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks first. Mac: click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Windows: click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.
  3. Pick a suite. Business, Growth or Home. Each keeps its files in its own folder, in Documents/Busy. You can move a folder into Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or iCloud Drive for backups and syncing.
  4. Make it yours. Your business name and logo, your career, your household. Then make your first invoice, log a win or add a contract.

Good to know

  • It’s a beta. Everything works, and your files are plain files in your own folders, so nothing is locked in. Tell us what you run into.
  • Updating? Download the new version and install it over the old one. Your folders are separate, so nothing is lost.
  • Your data stays on your computer and is never sent to us. The free app has no AI and doesn’t connect to the internet. Read the privacy page.
  • Can’t install apps? On Linux, a Chromebook or a work computer, use the browser version instead: Busy Business (zip), Busy Growth (zip) or Busy Home (zip). Unzip it and open it in Chrome or Edge.
