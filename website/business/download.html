How to start

  1. Install it. On a Mac, open the downloaded file and drag Busy Business into Applications. On Windows, run the downloaded file.
  2. Open it the first time. The beta isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks first. Mac: click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Windows: click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.
  3. Your business folder. Everything is saved as plain files in Documents/Busy/Business. You can choose another folder, for example inside Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, for backups and syncing.
  4. Tell us your business name. Plus your logo and bank details if you like. Then make your first invoice.

  • It’s a beta. Everything works, and your files are plain files in your own folder, so nothing is locked in. Tell us what you run into.
