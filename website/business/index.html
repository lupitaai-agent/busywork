Busy Business in short

What is Busy Business?

Busy Business is a free app for Mac and Windows with thirteen tools for a small business: a marketing studio and a brand kit, a project planner and a board, invoices and quotes, time, clients, documents, receipts, a money dashboard, subscriptions, trips and stock. Everything is saved in a folder on your own computer.

Who is it for?

Busy Business is for freelancers, shops, makers and small studios of up to about ten people, who run the business themselves and want one place for the brand, the projects and the admin.

What does it cost?

Busy Business is free, with no account and no subscription. Olaf, the AI brand consultant, comes in the paid edition: Busy Business with Olaf, $7.99 once on itch.io.

Is there AI in the free version?

No. The free Busy Business has no AI and sends nothing anywhere. Only Olaf, in the paid edition, uses AI: Claude, on your own Claude account.

Where is my data?

Busy Business saves plain files in a folder you choose on your own computer. Put the folder in Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive for backups and syncing. We have no copy and no way to see it.

Which languages?

Busy Business is in English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. Invoices, quotes and letters can be made in any of them.

Questions, answered

What is Olaf?

Olaf is the AI brand consultant in Busy Business with Olaf. He works out where your business stands, makes a 90-day plan, runs your projects with you and prepares the work each step needs: posts, review requests, your newsletter, your local listing. Olaf is built on proven marketing science. Works on your own Claude: the Claude app with a paid plan, or a Claude API key.

What is the difference between the free Marketing Studio and Olaf?

The Studio gives you the tools to do your own marketing: a brand profile, a year plan, a content calendar and insights from your invoices. You do the work, and there is no AI in it. Olaf is our AI brand consultant, right inside Busy: it works out where your business stands, makes a 90-day plan, checks in with you every week, and does the work each step needs. Both use the same marketing science.

Do I need Olaf to use the tools?

No. Every tool, the Marketing Studio included, is free and complete on its own. Olaf is for owners who would rather be guided than figure out their marketing alone.

Where does my data go when I use Olaf?

What Olaf reads goes to Anthropic under your own Claude account, and nowhere else. Your files stay in your own folder, and the free tools have no AI.

Can I use Google Drive?

Yes. Install Google Drive for desktop and pick a folder inside it. Drive backs it up and keeps it in sync across your computers.

Which computers?

Busy Business is an app for Mac and Windows, in beta. Without installing, a folder that runs in Chrome or Edge is there too. The demo on this site shows every tool with a pretend business.

Can I work on it with my team?

Share the folder through Google Drive and everyone sees the same clients and invoices. Team Mode is on the workbench.

Do the invoices meet the rules?

They include what EU invoices need: numbers that count up, dates, both parties, VAT per rate and your VAT number. Check with your accountant for anything special.
