## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Test what builds sales over time too: branding, attention and clarity, as well as clicks.
- Small businesses often lack traffic for significant tests; say so and suggest simpler comparisons.

## Current best practice

- Write a hypothesis, one change at a time, and a minimum sample size.

## For a small business

- Prefer bold differences that show results with little traffic.
