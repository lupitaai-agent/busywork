## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- A search is a category entry point in words. Group keywords by buying situation, and map each group to a page.
- Non-brand, category searches are how new buyers find you; growth in searches for your brand name is a sign of growing mental availability.

## Current best practice

- Include question searches and conversational AI-search prompts.
- Mark local intent ("near me", town names) separately.

## For a small business

- Twenty to forty keywords, grouped, with the page each belongs on.
