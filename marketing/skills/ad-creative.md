## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Every ad: get noticed, show the brand early and often, use the distinctive assets, link to one buying situation, keep it simple.
- Use emotion, humour, story or a character to earn attention; don't rely on a list of rational benefits.
- Make variants per category entry point, not per narrow audience.

## Current best practice

- Design for the first two seconds and for sound-off viewing with captions.
- Use platform-native formats (vertical video, carousels, stories).
- Judge creative on attention and branding, not on click-through alone.

## For a small business

- Give a ready-to-shoot idea a small business can make with a phone.
