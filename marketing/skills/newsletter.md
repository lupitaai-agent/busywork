# Newsletter

Goal: keep existing customers and contacts close, encourage repeat purchases, reviews and referrals.

## Principles
- A newsletter mostly reaches people who already know the business; it supports retention and word of mouth, not reach among new buyers. Keep it light and useful.
- Make it instantly recognisable: same name, colours, header, sign-off and structure every time.
- Link content to buying moments ("before the holidays", "spring check") so readers remember when to come back.

## Structure
1. A recognisable header and a personal opening from the owner (2–3 sentences).
2. One main story (useful tip, behind the scenes, a customer story).
3. One or two short items (news, seasonal moment, event).
4. One clear call to action.
5. A friendly sign-off and an easy way to forward it to a friend.

## Rules
Only send to people who agreed (GDPR); include unsubscribe; keep it short. Provide three subject line options and a preview line.
