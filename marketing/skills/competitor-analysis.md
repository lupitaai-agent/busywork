## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Compare mental availability (which buying situations they are linked to in content, ads and reviews) and physical availability (opening hours, Google Business Profile, booking, delivery, prices shown, reviews).
- Assume their customers are also your customers; look for buying situations they ignore.
- List their distinctive assets (colours, characters, slogans) so the business stays clearly different-looking.

## Current best practice

- Check how they appear in AI search answers and review platforms as well as in Google.
- Note review sentiment themes as purchase barriers or strengths.

## For a small business

- Keep it to the three to five competitors buyers really consider, and finish with five concrete actions.
