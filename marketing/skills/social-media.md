# Social media

Goal: use social media to stay visible and memorable, with content that is instantly recognisable as the business.

## Principles
- Organic posts mostly reach existing followers; reaching new buyers needs shareable content, collaborations, local groups and (small) paid boosts with broad local targeting.
- Every post carries the distinctive assets (colour, character, face, sound, format), so it's recognisable in the feed before anyone reads the name.
- Link posts to buying moments (CEPs) as well as to products.
- Consistency over volume: a rhythm the owner can keep for 12 months.

## Steps
1. Pick 1–2 platforms where the category's buyers already are.
2. Define 3–4 recurring formats ("Saturday fix of the week", "behind the counter", customer stories, seasonal moments). Recurring formats become distinctive assets themselves.
3. Make a monthly calendar with dates, format, CEP and caption.
4. Write captions in the brand voice with a first line that grabs attention; add alt text and captions on video.
5. Plan a small always-on boost for the best posts to reach non-followers in the area.

## Output
A calendar table and ready-to-post captions (and video scripts if asked). State the weekly time needed.
