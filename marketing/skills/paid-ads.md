## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Target broadly within the category and the area you serve; let the platform find category buyers. Avoid narrow interest stacks.
- Prefer an always-on budget all year over short bursts; reach beats frequency, so cap frequency.
- Judge success on reach among new buyers and new customers. Return on ad spend alone favours existing customers.

## Current best practice

- Use the platforms' automated campaigns with strong, branded creative.
- Set up conversion tracking and server-side events where possible.
- Run simple on/off area tests to see what is really incremental.

## For a small business

- Start small (for example €5–20 a day), keep it running, and review monthly.
