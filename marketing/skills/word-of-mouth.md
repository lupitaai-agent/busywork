# Word of mouth & reviews

Goal: more (genuine) positive talk, reviews and referrals, and good handling of negative talk.

## What the evidence says
- Word of mouth grows with the number of customers; growth drives talk more than talk drives growth. So word of mouth supports growth but doesn't replace reaching new buyers.
- Negative and positive word of mouth matter about equally per mention; handle complaints quickly and visibly.
- Reviews are part of prominence (physical availability) on maps and marketplaces.

## Steps
1. **Ask at the right moment**: right after a good experience, with a direct link (QR on receipts and invoices, a line in the thank-you email).
2. **Make sharing easy**: photo moments with the distinctive assets, short shareable stories, referral cards.
3. **Referral programme (optional)**: simple, rewarding both sides, no rewards for positive reviews specifically (not allowed).
4. **Reply to every review** in the brand voice; resolve negatives publicly and kindly.
5. **Reuse**: put real reviews (with permission where needed) on the website, in ads and on the counter.

## Output
Ready-to-use review request texts (in person, SMS, email), reply templates, and a 30-day plan.
