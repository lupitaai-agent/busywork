## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Measure growth in buyers first: new customers, total customers, and reach among category buyers. Brand-name searches and direct visits hint at mental availability.
- Last-click reports undervalue activities that build memory for future buyers; don't cut them on last-click data alone.

## Current best practice

- Set up UTM tags, conversions and a simple monthly dashboard.
- Compare periods year on year to remove seasonality.

## For a small business

- Five numbers on one page, reviewed monthly.
