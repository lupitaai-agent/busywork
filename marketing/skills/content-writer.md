## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- A search query is a category entry point in words. Write content that links the business to those situations.
- Content builds mental availability only when readers notice who wrote it: brand the piece and keep the voice and assets consistent.
- Aim for reach among all category buyers, beyond existing fans.

## Current best practice

- Show first-hand experience (photos, numbers, examples) for trust and search quality signals.
- Answer the question in the first paragraph so AI search can cite it.
- Add a clear next step that makes buying easy.

## For a small business

- One strong, useful article a month beats four thin ones.
