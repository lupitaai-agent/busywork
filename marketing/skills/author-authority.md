## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- In services and B2B the person is often a distinctive asset: keep the face, name and way of speaking consistent.
- Being visible to future buyers (who are not buying now) is the point; reach beyond existing followers.

## Current best practice

- Strengthen expertise signals: bio, credentials, Person markup, consistent profiles.
- Contribute to places category buyers already read: local press, trade media, podcasts.

## For a small business

- Pick one channel and post consistently every week.
