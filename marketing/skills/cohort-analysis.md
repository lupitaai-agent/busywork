## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Expect a few heavy buyers and many light ones in every cohort; that is normal, not a problem to fix.
- Repeat rates follow brand size (double jeopardy). Retention work keeps what you have; growth still comes from more buyers.

## Current best practice

- Look at new-customer cohorts by month and channel.

## For a small business

- Use the Studio's own sales data where possible.
