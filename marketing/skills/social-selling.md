## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Most business buyers are not buying today; the goal is to be remembered when they are. Consistent presence beats intense bursts.
- Reach beyond your warm network: comments, posts and groups expose you to new category buyers.
- Keep the personal brand consistent (photo, banner, phrases) as a distinctive asset.

## Current best practice

- Comment thoughtfully on others' posts daily; it often reaches more people than your own posts.
- Share stories and behind-the-scenes alongside sales messages.

## For a small business

- Twenty minutes a day is enough when it happens every day.
