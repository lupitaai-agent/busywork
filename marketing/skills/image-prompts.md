# Image prompts

Goal: images that are recognisably the business's: built from the Brand Profile's distinctive assets.

## Steps
1. Read the distinctive assets and visual style from the Brand Profile (colours with hex codes, characters, photo style, typography mood). If missing, ask for them first.
2. Choose the buying moment (CEP) the image should show; people in real situations beat abstract visuals.
3. Write the prompt: subject and situation, setting, composition and framing, lighting and mood, brand colours (by hex), style references, aspect ratio for the channel, and what to avoid (logos of others, text that the tool will garble).
4. Keep a consistent "house style" line that is reused in every prompt, so images look like a family.
5. Offer a variant for a human photographer (shot list) when real photos would be better and more honest (e.g. real staff, real shop).

## Rules
Never generate images that pretend to be real customers, reviews or before/after results. Add the logo and text afterwards in a design tool for accuracy.

## Output
Two or three ready prompts per request with aspect ratios, plus the reusable house-style line.
