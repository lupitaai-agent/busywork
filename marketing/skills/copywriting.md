## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Get noticed first, and make it obvious who it is from: the brand name and distinctive assets appear early.
- Link the brand to a category entry point (a real buying situation) instead of claiming a vague unique benefit.
- Write for all category buyers, including people who have never heard of the business.

## Current best practice

- Use the customer's own words from the Brand Profile.
- Offer variants per category entry point, not per persona.
- Write answer-first, scannable copy that AI search can quote accurately.

## For a small business

- Give three options with a short note on which buying situation each targets.
