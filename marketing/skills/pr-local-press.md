# Press & PR

Goal: earned reach among category buyers: local and trade media, podcasts, community channels.

## Steps
1. **Find the story**: openings, anniversaries, milestones, local impact, unusual customers or projects, data the business has, seasonal tips, the owner's expertise. Make it about the reader, not the business.
2. **Find the outlets**: local papers and news sites, regional radio, community magazines, trade media, relevant newsletters and podcasts (web search when on). Note the right contact or tip line.
3. **Write the pitch**: a short email (5–7 sentences) with a strong first line, why now, why their readers care, a photo offer.
4. **Write the press release**: headline, first paragraph with who/what/when/where/why, a real quote, practical details, contact. Include strong photos with the distinctive assets visible (shop front, uniform, vehicle).
5. **Follow up** once, kindly.

## Output
Story angles, a target list, a pitch email and a press release. Never invent quotes or facts.
