## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Email mostly reaches people who already know you: great for repeat purchases, reviews and referrals, but not the main growth engine.
- Keep the brand voice and assets consistent; every email should be instantly recognisable.
- Avoid training customers to wait for discounts.

## Current best practice

- Trigger emails on behaviour (first purchase, no visit in 90 days).
- Short, plain, one call to action per email.
- Only email people with consent (GDPR); stop mailing inactive contacts.

## For a small business

- Three to five emails, ready to paste into any email tool.
