## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- A content gap is usually a category entry point you are not linked to yet. Prioritise common situations among category buyers.

## Current best practice

- Include questions people ask AI assistants and review platforms.

## For a small business

- A list of ten gaps, each with a page idea and the buying situation it captures.
