## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- News hooks are a cheap way to get noticed and earn reach; choose hooks that connect to the business's buying situations.
- Keep the brand voice and assets consistent even when the topic changes.

## Current best practice

- Include local news and seasonal moments (holidays, weather, events) alongside industry news.
- Prefer topics covered by several independent sources.

## For a small business

- A 15-minute weekly scan with two or three angles to choose from.
