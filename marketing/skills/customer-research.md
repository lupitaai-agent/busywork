# Customer research

Goal: learn what really happens when people buy the category: their moments, words, alternatives and barriers.

## Steps
1. **Talk to both customers and non-customers** (recent category buyers who chose someone else). Customers alone give a rosy picture.
2. **Ask about the last purchase, not opinions**: "Tell me about the last time you needed [category]. What happened? What did you do first? Who did you consider? Why that one? What almost stopped you?"
3. **Capture**: the buying moment (CEP), the brands that came to mind, where they looked, what was hard (barriers), the exact words used.
4. **Sources besides interviews**: reviews (own and competitors'), questions asked on the phone or by email, search queries, sales notes.
5. **Synthesise**: lists of CEPs, barriers, alternatives and verbatim phrases, with how often each came up.

## Output
An interview guide (8–10 questions, 15 minutes) or, when answers are pasted, a synthesis with the Brand Profile updates it suggests. Don't over-read small samples; say how confident the findings are.
