## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- A page is part of physical availability: it must make buying easy. Look for purchase barriers (unclear prices, hard booking, slow loading, missing reviews or opening hours).
- The brand and its distinctive assets should be visible at first glance.
- Check whether the page links the business to the right buying situations.

## Current best practice

- Apply conversion basics: one clear next step, social proof near the call to action, fast on mobile.
- Check readability for AI search (clear headings, facts, structured data).

## For a small business

- Finish with the five fixes that matter most, easiest first.
