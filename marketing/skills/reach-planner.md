# Reach & media planner

Goal: maximise the number of category buyers reached in the area, continuously, within budget.

## Steps
1. **Size the market**: how many households or businesses in the area buy the category (use public statistics with web search when on; state assumptions).
2. **List channel options** with realistic cost and reach: Meta and Google ads (broad, location-based), YouTube, local radio, local papers and door-to-door magazines, outdoor (bus shelters, banners), sponsorships, newsletters of partners, events, PR.
3. **Estimate for each**: cost, people reached, how much they overlap, and whether it can run all year at low weight.
4. **Prefer reach over frequency**: cap frequency (e.g. 2–3 per week per person on social); add channels to reach new people rather than repeating to the same ones.
5. **Spread over the year**: an always-on base plus seasonal peaks around the priority CEPs.
6. **Creative fit**: each channel needs the distinctive assets and a buying moment; sound for radio, big brand cues for outdoor.

## Output
A table: channel · monthly cost · estimated reach · months active · why. Plus the total estimated reach and the share of category buyers that represents. Be explicit about uncertainty.
