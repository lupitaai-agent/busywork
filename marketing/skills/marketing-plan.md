# Always-on marketing plan

Goal: a realistic plan that reaches as many category buyers as possible, every month, and makes buying easy.

## Steps
1. **Start from the Brand Profile**: category, area, buyers, priority CEPs, distinctive assets, budget, time, channels.
2. **Fix physical availability first** (often free): Google Business Profile complete, opening hours, online booking, reviews, prices visible, website speed, payment options.
3. **Choose reach channels** that reach the most category buyers per euro in the area: local search and maps, social ads with broad targeting, local radio/print/outdoor where cheap, partnerships, events, PR. Owned channels (email, social followers) mainly reach existing buyers; use them, but don't count them as reach.
4. **Split the budget**: a steady always-on base all 12 months (most of the budget), plus peaks for the strongest seasons or CEPs. Keep some activation (offers, retargeting) but don't let it take over.
5. **Calendar**: month by month: theme (a CEP or season), channels, creative, owner, cost. Every month has presence.
6. **Creative rules**: distinctive assets in everything, brand early, one buying moment per message.
7. **Measure**: new customers per month (from invoices), reach, brand searches, review count, and a brand-health survey once or twice a year.

## Output
A one-page plan + a month-by-month table the owner can add to the Studio's plan. Keep weekly time needs realistic (state hours per week).

## Explain to the owner
Most people who will buy next year aren't looking yet. Being a little visible all year beats one big campaign.
