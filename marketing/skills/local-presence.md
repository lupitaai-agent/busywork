# Local presence (physical availability)

Goal: when someone nearby needs the category, the business is present, relevant and prominent.

## Checklist
**Presence**
- Google Business Profile claimed, verified, correct category (primary + secondary), service area, opening hours incl. holidays, phone, website, booking link.
- Apple Maps, Bing Places, and the main local/sector directories and marketplaces buyers use.
- Name, address and phone identical everywhere.

**Relevance**
- Services and products listed with prices or "from" prices; attributes (wheelchair access, parking, payment methods, languages).
- Photos that show the place, the people and the distinctive assets (colours, signage, vehicle), at least monthly.
- Q&A answered with real buying-moment questions.

**Prominence**
- Steady flow of genuine reviews (ask every happy customer; reply to all, kindly, including negative ones).
- Posts/updates weekly on the profile.
- Local links and mentions (associations, suppliers, local media).

## Output
A scored checklist (done / to do), the top five fixes, and ready-to-paste texts (business description, service descriptions, review request message, replies to recent reviews if pasted).

## Honesty
Never buy or fake reviews or offer rewards for positive ones; it breaks platform rules and consumer law.
