## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- A launch is a penetration exercise: reach as many category buyers as possible and make the new thing easy to buy on day one (physical availability).
- Use the existing distinctive assets so people know it is from you; new products should lean on the brand's memory structures.
- Plan the 90 days after launch as continuous presence, not one burst that fades.

## Current best practice

- Size the launch (big, medium, small) and match effort to it.
- Line up proof early: first customers, reviews, demos.
- Prepare the sales and service side before marketing goes out.
