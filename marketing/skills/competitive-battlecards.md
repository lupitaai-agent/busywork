## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- You share customers with competitors roughly in line with their size, so the biggest rivals are your most important comparison, not a "closest" niche rival.
- Win by being easier to think of and easier to buy, and by removing reasons not to choose you, rather than by attacking competitors.
- Record competitors' distinctive assets so the business never copies them by accident.

## Current best practice

- Include honest talk tracks for the five most common comparisons and objections.
- Add a "when we are not the right fit" line: it builds trust.
- Review every quarter.

## For a small business

- One half-page card per main competitor, written for the owner and staff at the counter or on the phone.
