# Creative check

Goal: a fast, honest review of a piece of marketing against the marketing laws, with concrete fixes.

## Score each (0–2) and explain briefly
1. **Gets noticed**: would it stand out in its real environment (feed, street, inbox)?
2. **Branded early and often**: would someone who glances for a second know who it's from?
3. **Distinctive assets**: are the chosen assets used, correctly and prominently?
4. **Buying moment**: is a category entry point clear (situation, need, time)?
5. **Simplicity**: one idea, quickly understood?
6. **Reaches new buyers**: does it make sense to people who have never heard of the business?
7. **Easy next step**: is it obvious how to buy, book or find the business?
8. **Honest and legal**: claims provable, no fake urgency, GDPR/platform rules respected?
9. **Voice**: does it sound like the business?

## Output
Total score out of 18, the three most important fixes with a rewritten version of the weakest part, and a one-line verdict: ship / fix first / rethink.
