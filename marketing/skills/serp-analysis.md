## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- Search results are a shelf: being prominent there is physical availability online. Check the map pack, reviews, AI answers and ads as well as the blue links.

## Current best practice

- Note which result types win (maps, videos, questions, AI overview) and plan content in that format.

## For a small business

- Check five searches that bring real customers, not vanity terms.
