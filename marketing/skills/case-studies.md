## How this skill applies the marketing laws

- A good story shows the buying situation (category entry point) that brought the customer, so readers recognise their own moment.
- Name the business early and use its distinctive assets in every format.
- Stories amplify word of mouth; share them widely, well beyond existing customers.

## Current best practice

- Make short versions: a quote card, a 30-second video script and a review-style snippet.
- Get written approval and keep numbers real.

## For a small business

- Never invent results or quotes. If something is missing, write the question to ask the customer.
